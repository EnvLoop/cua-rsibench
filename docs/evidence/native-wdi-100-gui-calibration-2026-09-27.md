# Original WDI/LibreOffice final-candidate GUI calibration: 100/100

Audited 2026-09-27. This is a **full-size environment/task qualification result**, not a Qwen benchmark score. The provisional `desktop-native` primary source is the original EnvLoop LibreOffice task factory over a pinned [World Development Indicators](https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037712/world-development-indicators) snapshot ([CC BY 4.0](https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/public-licenses)): 35 countries, five indicators, 2019–2024, and 1,050 nonmissing authentic observations. The analyst cases, policy thresholds, Office documents, and oracles are authored simulations, clearly separated from those historical observations. Train/selection/final use 5/5/25 nonoverlapping country source families and substantively different one-/two-/three-edit task structures. The 100 private final candidates comprise **50 Calc, 25 Impress, and 25 Writer** tasks.

The [aggregate-only audit](native-wdi-100-gui-calibration-2026-09-27.json) re-read every private package, original input, actor-saved output, screenshot hash, evaluator receipt, and cold reset. **100/100** final candidates each have a native-GUI saved positive, a plausible saved near-miss rejected for target-only reasons, and an exact-input restoration in a distinct fresh sandbox. The 300 accepted control attempts use **300 distinct E2B Desktop sandbox identities**; their provider template, LibreOffice binary probe, and visible resource class are consistent. All **25/25** final Impress inputs underwent a separate trusted neutral LibreOffice save before actor attempts, with slide text and drawable structure preserved and geometry subsequently guarded. No private final task text, source-country mapping, oracle, screenshot, raw sandbox ID, or credential is in the public aggregate.

Failures were retained and audited separately. A prior E2B control-plane interruption produced **16** pre-action `ConnectError` attempts, including no-ID cases; their server leases and a grace period elapsed, a separate bounded health probe passed, and one controlled retry per archived attempt was allowed. The [interruption record](native-wdi-e2b-interruption-2026-09-25.md) preserves their provenance and never labels them model failures. A partial Impress positive, two Calc focus failures, and two Writer search-focus near-misses were also independently rejected, preserved, and repaired only through fresh GUI attempts; see the [Impress](native-wdi-impress-gui-timing-recovery-2026-09-25.md), [Calc](native-wdi-calc-focus-recovery-2026-09-25.md), and [Writer](native-wdi-writer-find-recovery-2026-09-27.md) controls. These historical failures do not inflate the 100-task accepted denominator.

The lane-wide [lease ledger](native-wdi-100-gui-calibration-2026-09-27.json) counts **353** GUI-create attempts or shared neutral/health batches, including failed calls. Fourteen create attempts had **no acknowledged sandbox ID**; their original receipts remain preserved and their bounded leases were allowed to expire before controlled recovery. The ledger's **$31.9278** figure is a conservative **full-server-lease planning reserve** at $1/hour, below the fixed $40 lane cap; it is **not** an invoice or paid amount. The observed desktop class is 8 vCPU / 8 GiB, for which the [published E2B marginal compute price](https://e2b.dev/pricing) is $0.5328/hour before credits or plan charges. Actual provider billing, discounts, credits, and subscription charges remain **unknown** in the SDK evidence. Two early normalization batches had under-reserved $0.35/hour assumptions; their original receipts remain untouched and the [cost correction](native-wdi-e2b-cost-correction-2026-09-25.md) explicitly invalidates those dollar upper-bound claims.
