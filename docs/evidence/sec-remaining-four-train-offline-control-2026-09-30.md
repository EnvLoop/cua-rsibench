The four issuer-distinct bundles come from the previously [captured and identity-audited SEC TRAIN source pool](sec-train-22-raw-source-capture-2026-09-29.md). Two original 2025 10-Ks support full international-pension asset movement. Their same-plan opening assets, acquisitions or divestitures where reported, returns, employer and participant contributions, currency or other reported activity, benefits, settlements, and closing assets reconcile for both filed years. Closing assets less projected benefit obligation also matches each filed funded status. One issuer's fiscal years close in late December rather than on December 31; the authored workbook preserves its exact fiscal dates and duration contexts. One issuer reports currency activity together with other items, while the other reports foreign-exchange changes separately. An unreported acquisition is displayed as `n.r.` in source cells and treated as zero only in the authored bridge; it is not labeled a filed zero.

The other two filings support available-for-sale securities. For both 2024 and 2025, amortized cost plus signed credit-loss allowance, unrealized gains, and signed unrealized losses rebuilds filed fair value. The loss-aging buckets reconcile to the **reported loss-position subset**, which one issuer discloses more narrowly than total gross unrealized losses. The current maturity-bucket fair values reconcile to the same AFS fair-value total. The maturity cost basis differs across issuers: one filing reports cost net of the credit-loss allowance, and the other reports gross amortized cost. Both bases are labeled and reconciled in their own workbook. No loss subset or maturity-basis amount was invented to force a uniform table.
