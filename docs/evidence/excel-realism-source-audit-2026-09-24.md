# Hard Excel workbook source audit (2026-09-24)

## Decision

Use **SpreadsheetBench 2 Financial_Model and Debugging** as the first hard-task *candidate pool* for Microsoft Excel for the web: 100 tasks in each category, 200 total. Inspection of the released manifests shows that these 200 inputs share only **30 golden workbook families** (20 financial models and 10 debugging bases). This is 200 task instances, not 200 independent company models. The published task counts are not a count of web-compatible, GUI-solvable, or independently verified admissions. Keep SpreadsheetBench V1/Verified and ExcelForum workbooks as secondary variety, after selecting for multi-sheet structure and a meaningful workflow. A single simple SpreadsheetBench task is an infrastructure smoke test, not evidence of realistic Excel competence.

## Direct inspection of released files

Downloaded the official [`spreadsheetbench-v2.zip`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/KAKA22/SpreadsheetBench-v2/blob/main/spreadsheetbench-v2.zip) at Hugging Face revision `5a2215ed4121945ab09d8723df2995602090b042`. The 134,207,025-byte archive has SHA-256 `17147ef9578cd57ce76c9a719d19da7821f3e5cb0d8f776c820f699fdcdb761c`. All four `dataset.json` manifests were parsed locally: 100 Financial_Model inputs / 20 gold paths (exactly five tasks per gold), 100 Debugging inputs / 10 gold paths (exactly ten tasks per gold), 97 Template inputs / 97 gold paths, and 24 Visualization inputs / 24 gold paths. The 30 Financial_Model/Debugging gold files have 30 distinct SHA-256 hashes, establishing 30 distinct files; deeper template-lineage deduplication remains to be done.

The actual Financial_Model `01_01` input (`spreadsheet/01_Project Education/01_01_Education_input.xlsx`, SHA-256 `a01657c0cc7116569be1d649bb259d32073d3fa6261d7d9da22c461875e1080c`) has 25 sheets, 223,619 instantiated cells (8,835 nonempty), 3,943 formula cells, 31 merged ranges, and one data validation rule. Its instruction asks for revenue CAGR, cross-tab revenue drivers, course cost lines, working-capital roll-forward, depreciation and balance-sheet checks. The input and shared gold differ in 82 formula/value cells across six sheets. This is a credible multi-stage visible-UI task if the file loads and saves in Excel for the web; the 223,619-cell styled extent may slow that UI. The input has two VML drawing parts that its gold lacks, so raw ZIP equality to the gold is inappropriate.

The Debugging `01_01` input (`spreadsheet/01_Debugging/input_files/Double Counting_input.xlsx`, SHA-256 `e6d8eb78c3c91d352c054a5c043493861954ffa1cf75f80076a1b2d062709c10`) has 14 sheets, 4,685 instantiated cells (3,585 nonempty), 1,837 formulas and 51 merged ranges. The deliberately vague task says to audit the file without disclosing target cells. A raw comparison to its gold shows 123 formula/value changes: 100 involve `TABLE(...)` sensitivity-table formulas or cached text representation, 22 are substantive non-table formula changes in LBO and M&A areas, and one is equivalent array-formula range metadata (`C36` versus `C36:C36`). The input has 51 merged ranges versus 88 in the gold, and drawing parts differ. This is a **web round-trip and oracle-calibration red flag**, not proof the browser fails. A verifier must distinguish the injected fault from source/gold conversion artifacts before admission.

Neither of these two sampled inputs contains OOXML external-link, connection, pivot, chart or VBA parts. That observation does not establish the same for the other files. No Microsoft account, upload, GUI execution, or Excel-web compatibility test was used in this inspection.

The official V1 [`spreadsheetbench_912_v0.1.tar.gz`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/KAKA22/SpreadsheetBench/blob/main/spreadsheetbench_912_v0.1.tar.gz) was also downloaded (SHA-256 `9cf7228b54f1edcdd4b372eb736774adf29cb4f804c9920229bac6c154833399`). For each of its 912 tasks, the first input XLSX package was inspected: 912 distinct content hashes; 674 single-sheet, 164 two-sheet, 38 three-sheet, 21 four-sheet, and 15 with five or more sheets. Counting both explicit and shared-follower OOXML formula elements, 35 have at least two sheets and 100 existing formula cells; 28 of those contain at least one explicit cross-sheet formula. Only 15 have at least three sheets and 100 formulas. The median formula count is zero and the 90th percentile is 73. This is a structural prefilter, not proof those 35 tasks are hard: spot-checking their prompts found simple cell-formatting instructions and VBA-dependent requests among them.

**100 distinct hard workbook families remain unproven.** Under even the loose `>=2 sheets AND >=100 formulas` prefilter, V2's 30 base families plus at most 35 V1 first-input families yield no more than 65 candidates before overlap, Excel-web compatibility, solvability, licensing, and verifier review. The more demanding `>=3 sheets AND >=100 formulas` screen yields at most 45. V1 multi-sheet data tasks with few existing formulas may still be hard in a different way, and the separate 5,925 ExcelForum tasks in Spreadsheet-RL could add candidates, but those have not been scanned for family uniqueness or difficulty. A 100-family final set therefore requires further file-level admission and probably new expert-built workbook families.

The [SEC EDGAR companyfacts API](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces) offers public, unauthenticated extracted XBRL financial facts and filing metadata that can ground new distinct issuer/year models. This is a source for authoring original workbooks and hidden perturbations, **not** a ready-made 100-task workbook benchmark. Preserve CIK, accession, fiscal period, unit and filing date, and review any copied filing text or layout before redistributing workbooks. The SEC documents public access; that access alone is not evidence that every issuer-created document can be republished under a benchmark license.

## License and redistribution boundary

The [SpreadsheetBench 2 paper, Appendix A.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.29955) says the **dataset is CC BY-SA 4.0** and evaluation code is MIT. The [Hugging Face dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/KAKA22/SpreadsheetBench-v2) labels that same dataset **MIT**. The conflict is unresolved; use the more restrictive CC BY-SA terms for internal assessment and do not repackage the files for a public release until the publisher clarifies. The V1 [dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/KAKA22/SpreadsheetBench) labels its data CC BY-SA 4.0. The Spreadsheet-RL [card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Spreadsheet-RL/Spreadsheet-RL) also labels its aggregate CC BY-SA 4.0; this does not eliminate the need to track each underlying source.

## Verifier implications

The V2 [published evaluator](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RUCKBReasoning/SpreadsheetBench-2/main/evaluation/evaluation.py) takes `answer_position` from task metadata and compares only enumerated cells inside those ranges. Its numeric comparison allows 1% relative error or 0.01 absolute error near zero; it rounds the regression fraction to four digits and promotes values at least 0.998 to 1.0. It can therefore miss edits outside the answer ranges. This implementation is narrower than the [paper's prose](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.29955) that says every unchanged cell must remain unchanged. V2 also [recalculates with LibreOffice](https://github.com/RUCKBReasoning/SpreadsheetBench-2/blob/main/README.md) before scoring. Its 24 chart tasks use a VLM checklist and [Windows Excel/WPS COM image export](https://github.com/RUCKBReasoning/SpreadsheetBench-2/blob/main/README.md), which is not a deterministic, browser-only verifier.

For a Microsoft Excel computer-use benchmark, the actor should receive only the input workbook and task instruction via the visible application. The independent evaluator should download the saved workbook and inspect the OOXML package without trusting the actor's narration or a screenshot. At minimum it should check:

1. Expected outputs after Excel's own recalculation, with numeric tolerances specified per domain and cell, plus representative hidden input perturbations to reject hardcoded answers.
2. Formula versus constant requirements and relative-reference propagation for target ranges; cross-sheet dependency and error-code behavior where the task requires them. Allow semantically equivalent formulas only when hidden perturbations establish equivalent results.
3. All non-target cells and workbook structure for no-regression: sheet identities/order, defined names, tables, validations, styles, charts, and relevant drawing parts. Normalize only documented Office save churn, such as caches and document metadata, after observing an untouched web round-trip.
4. Chart task data ranges, series, axes, titles and types from OOXML, followed by visual review when appearance matters. Do not make a VLM verdict the sole success signal.

Every trial starts from a versioned input copy with a checksum. The evaluator reads a separate saved/downloaded artifact; the gold file and scoring ranges remain outside the actor workspace. Split train/selection/final by original workbook family and source document, not task ID. Spreadsheet-RL already republishes all 297 V2 non-visual instances, so original public V2 gold files cannot serve as a genuinely hidden final exam.

## Excel-for-web admission gate

Microsoft says [some workbooks cannot be edited in Excel for the web](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/excel/edit-a-workbook-that-contains-features-unsupported-by-excel-for-the-web) and documents differences in [browser workbook behavior](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/excel/differences-between-using-a-workbook-in-the-browser-and-in-excel). For each candidate, require these recorded checks before counting it as benchmark-ready:

1. **Static inspection:** valid `.xlsx`; source SHA-256; sheet count, visible/hidden sheets, used ranges, formula count/types, merged regions, charts, pivots, external links, connections, macros, XML maps, protected sheets, and embedded objects. Flag unsupported or externally dependent features, and do not silently strip them.
2. **Untouched browser round-trip:** upload a copy, open in *edit* mode, save, download, and compare input/output OOXML. Reject if Excel changes task-critical formulas, values, charts, or references on its own. Record load and save time.
3. **Human GUI solution:** a reviewer using only visible Excel UI completes the task and saves it under the proposed action/time budget. Large fill ranges are acceptable through normal UI operations; tasks requiring hundreds of individual clicks are not.
4. **Independent oracle:** the human solution passes the stricter verifier; intentionally wrong formulas, edits to unrelated cells, missing target regions, and stale cached values fail. Test a second hidden input variant.
5. **Reset and isolation:** fresh copy/version for every attempt, distinct file IDs, no gold/answer range in actor-accessible folders, and a reproducible checksum manifest.

As documented by Microsoft, VBA cannot [run or be edited in Excel for the web](https://support.microsoft.com/en-US/Excel/work-with-vba-macros-in-excel-for-the-web); protected sheets may be view-only; XML maps and some external-data-linked features affect browser viewing/editing. These are per-file compatibility checks, not blanket claims that all formulas, charts or pivots fail on the web. The V2 visualization category is a separate admission track; the initial >=100 hard-task target can be pursued from the 200 Financial_Model/Debugging candidates alone.

## Hard task shapes to select

1. **Multi-statement forecast and valuation:** fill 5-year income statement, balance sheet, cash flow and DCF from an assumptions sheet and historical filings; preserve row conventions and reconcile working-capital, debt and terminal value across 10-40 tabs. Prefer tasks with coherent fill blocks and dependency checks over one-cell questions. [V2 example and statistics](https://spreadsheetbench.github.io/).
2. **Blind model audit:** locate injected double-counting, wrong sign, shifted range, broken lookup and hardcoded-value faults across 15-99 sheets with no disclosed error coordinates; repair the causal formulas and leave unaffected formulas/styles intact. [V2 task construction and error taxonomy](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.29955).
3. **Source-to-model reconciliation:** update a model from multiple disclosed statement/segment tables, handle fiscal-year alignment and units, then prove total revenue, margins, cash flow and valuation reconcile. Select from the V2 Financial_Model pool; the specific cases and required source visibility must be checked file by file. [V2 source description](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.29955).
4. **Irregular forum workbook repair:** select V1/ExcelForum attachments with nested headers, interleaved tables, narrative notes, formatting-dependent meaning and multiple test workbooks; require a formula or transformation that survives changed values. [V1 paper](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/file/ac840df270ac537dd74530a15c332684-Paper-Datasets_and_Benchmarks_Track.pdf).
5. **Presentation deliverable, later track:** combo/secondary-axis charts and pivot-based summaries sourced from irregular tables, with structure and rendered-view checks. V2 has only 24 visualization cases and its published VLM/COM scoring cannot by itself supply our deterministic Excel-web oracle. [V2 example](https://spreadsheetbench.github.io/).

## Evidence status

The sources above were read from the official paper, repository, dataset pages, evaluator source, and Microsoft support pages on 2026-09-24. The 200 hard V2 cases are **published candidates**, not an admitted Excel-for-web set. No user account, workbook upload, GUI run, or empirical compatibility pass is claimed in this document.
