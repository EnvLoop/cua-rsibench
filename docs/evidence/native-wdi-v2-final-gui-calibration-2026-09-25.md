# Original WDI desktop v2: private final-ID GUI calibration

Checked 2026-09-25. This is **evaluator-only pre-result task calibration**, not a Qwen score, a checkpoint result, or a 100-task benchmark. It follows the [pre-result desktop source amendment](../FULL_STUDY_DESKTOP_SOURCE_AMENDMENT_2026-09-25.md). The underlying country-year numbers are a frozen, licensed [World Development Indicators](https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037712/world-development-indicators) snapshot (35 countries × five indicators × six years, SHA-256 `cad6aafbd856ebb78f3f50106ab1b5b38ac369a2da251e2442268e296fde6d9f`; [CC BY 4.0](https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/public-licenses)). EnvLoop authors the analyst scenarios, decision policies, layouts, and oracles.

The first candidate design reused the same four structural templates across train, selection, and final. The [v2 factory](../../native_desktop_factory/factory_v2.py) instead implements **actual structural and causal differences**, rather than merely renaming template IDs:

| Application | Train: exposed basic control | Selection: held-out country families | Final candidates: held-out country families |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calc | Two-sheet, one-edit review | Three-sheet, two-factor review | Four-sheet, three-formula decision case with a separate simulated policy tab and evidence/provenance tabs |
| Impress | Four-slide, one-signal deck | Five-slide, two-signal review | Seven-slide, three-signal committee case with separate output, price/labor, validation, and action slides |
| Writer | One source table, one finding | Three-year source table, two findings | Six-year source table plus simulated decision-rule table, three findings |

Source countries remain disjoint: five train, five selection, and 25 final families. Four workflows per country yield **20 / 20 / 100 candidates**, with 50 Calc, 25 Impress, and 25 Writer final files. The private country/defect split is withheld; the public [field-limited receipt](native-wdi-v2-final-gui-calibration-2026-09-25.json) binds its digest and the current candidate-inventory SHA-256 `c5b6aff9c3d35bb2f9dba980fec7aa9693fd744a13dc3aebe185e4831c029f98`. The factory regenerated byte-identically from the same source and private map before native normalization. A static sweep also rejected an edge case in which one country's wrong unemployment statement matched the correct value after rounding; the v2 generator now chooses a genuinely different earlier observation or an explicit fallback. A separate scan confirms that all 100 final candidates have three target formulas or text values different from their oracle targets, though this alone does not prove behavioral difficulty.

**Four** private final candidate identities, drawn from **two** distinct country source families, passed their full task-specific **GUI positive / plausible near-miss / fresh cold-reset** calibration: two Calc, one Impress, and one Writer. Each positive used the visible native GUI to repair all three required fields, saved in place, and passed independent parsing of the saved XLSX/PPTX/DOCX before evaluator postprocessing. Each near-miss used the GUI and saved a plausible wrong third field; the independent verifier rejected only target errors while preserving unrelated source cells, slide objects/layout, or paragraphs/tables. Each reset used a distinct new E2B sandbox and restored the exact candidate input hash; all 12 actor-control sandboxes were observed stopped after termination. Task IDs, countries, instructions, oracles, screenshots, and full receipts remain private; the public JSON exposes opaque HMAC references and hashes only. These edits were scripted known controls, **not model attempts**.

The Impress candidate first underwent a separate, neutral LibreOffice save in its own sandbox. Original and normalized slide texts/drawable structures were identical; the normalized input was rebound into the private candidate inventory before any actor attempt. The actor-positive and near-miss then used separate fresh sandboxes. The stronger OOXML verifier checks slide order, canvas, all text/drawable counts, target text, other text, and non-target geometry within 0.02 in. **Only 1/25** final Impress candidate inputs has this normalization receipt. The other 24 remain unqualified until their source packages undergo the same check and their own actor controls.

The offline per-ID GUI gate reports **4/100 calibrated**, **96 missing**, **0 invalid**. This count is a narrower engineering milestone. **Official full-study admitted final tasks remain 0/100; official student/model results remain 0.** The full E2B image/font/profile digest, final Qwen3.8 screenshot/action/sampling contract, per-ID legal/runtime/cost budget, all 100 task receipts, and hidden pre-campaign freeze have not been established. The v2 design has greater action/reasoning demand than the exposed one-edit tasks, but no model study has yet demonstrated that it is discriminating. No researcher may use final calibration feedback to revise training or choose a checkpoint.
