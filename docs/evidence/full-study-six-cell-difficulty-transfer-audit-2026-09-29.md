# Six-cell difficulty and transfer audit (source-only, pre-result)

**Status:** design audit, not a model-difficulty measurement, admission, campaign, or result. This note reads public aggregate receipts and task-factory source only. It does not inspect hidden final IDs, task text, answers, or gold. As of this audit, the [study protocol](../FULL_STUDY_PREREGISTRATION.md) reports **0/600 official final admissions, 0/24 researcher campaigns, and 0/3,000 matched final outcomes**. The planned 100 tasks per cell do not establish that the base student has room to improve or that data-only LoRA training can transfer.

The key distinction is between a *source-family split* (different countries, issuers, projects, products, or business entities) and a *causal-workflow split* (different rules and required action sequences). Both can be valuable, but they answer different questions. A final rule absent from training can test extrapolation; it cannot be presented as demonstrated learning transfer until train-only model evidence supports that claim. The risks below are predictions from task design, **not observed Qwen, Sol, or Astra performance**.

## Pre-result decision, before expensive final controls

1. **Freeze a transfer matrix now.** For every final workflow, record the exact train and selection *skill analogue* (or mark `unseen_extrapolation`), target-field count, causal decisions, distractor objects, native screen transitions, and independent no-regression checks. Publish only aggregate counts and code hashes; keep task IDs, source allocations and gold private. An analogous *skill* may use a different source/layout/template, so source/entity/template disjointness and comparable reasoning demand can coexist. The six current 20-task train pools do not cover every final skill at final-like depth.
2. **Amend training sources before results if effect is the priority.** Give all four researcher configurations the same additional train-only source/task generation capacity or rebalance the fixed 20 anchors. Preserve the original pools and exact pre-result amendment. For GitLab and Magento, four final workflow types can fit the 20 anchors with several source families each; PPT needs ten final workflow types and Excel has 13 final graphs, so 20 anchors alone cannot provide robust within-workflow transfer evidence. Do not use final or selection source families to create training analogues. If an intentionally unseen rule remains, label it a separate extrapolation stratum in the report.
3. **Run the existing paired train-only difficulty screen before the 100-case model run.** Its frozen heuristic needs eight valid Qwen/reference pairs from at least two train source families *per represented workflow*, with matched action/wall limits, independent saved-state score and reset. Covering every current final workflow at that threshold would require at least **80 PPT, 104 Excel, 32 Desktop, 32 Odoo, 40 GitLab, and 32 Magento train-only paired task instances** (320 total) if all listed graph/family types remain. These are *diagnostic requirements*, not extra final tasks or an observed cost. Start with two representative cases per cell to separate action-contract failures from task difficulty; retain all attempts. The [screen](../TRAIN_ONLY_DIFFICULTY_CALIBRATION_2026-09-27.md) explicitly rejects ceiling, floor, and infrastructure-invalid patterns without converting them to model scores.
4. **Report effect at the source-family and workflow level.** The 100 raw final identities are correlated within 25 PPT countries, 25 Desktop countries, 20 GitLab projects, four repeated Odoo causal families, and four repeated Magento policies. Excel's exact final issuer-family count is not exposed in the aggregate, though no issuer contributes more than five offline cases. Predefine family-aware intervals and paired base/selected discordance; publish valid denominators, invalid infrastructure outcomes, and data-source tiers. Passing evaluator positive/negative/reset controls proves solvability and verifiability, not model discrimination.

No workflow should be removed, relaxed, or rewritten in response to an official final model outcome. A source-only amendment and train-only probe must precede the campaign freeze; after freeze, report the observed ceiling or floor honestly.
