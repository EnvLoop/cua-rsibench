# Odoo causal-rule QA before hidden final generation

**Status, 2026-09-25:** The evaluator tested a new set of four Odoo business-rule templates with an **evaluator-private QA seed**. All 100 QA candidates passed individual native-GUI source readback, known-answer correction, independent saved-state positive, wrong-object negative and exact cold PostgreSQL/physical-filestore restoration. This is a software and verifier qualification result, **not a hidden final score**. Official final tasks admitted: **0/100**; model and researcher attempts: **0**.

The rules differ in actual decision or action requirements from the 100 exposed development-calibration tasks. Purchase must choose the current supplier acknowledgement over a superseded revision and edit the vendor reference in addition to the lines. Inventory must compute service-level stock from mean demand, demand range, lead time and a service factor instead of the old recency-weighted seasonal rule. Sales must set a quotation expiration from the customer PO deadline. CRM must replace unverified intake email and phone values with confirmed handoff contacts. The original Odoo Community GUI exposes all added fields. The read-only evaluator scores those persisted fields and rejects unrelated edits and new business records.

The [machine-readable QA receipt](odoo-hidden-causal-qa-2026-09-25.json) provides aggregate counts and digests of the private per-case run and independent audit. It contains no task prompts, source values, gold, credentials or QA seed. The private audit checked an exclusive worker lease, actor permissions, evaluator read-only access, package/baseline bindings and source-file hashes. The QA worker is stopped and cannot be promoted into the hidden denominator.

A fresh final seed must be generated **after** this QA, with separate source entities and documents. Its combined 20 train / 20 selection / 100 official-candidate identity manifest must pass the existing v0.6 split validator and an explicit comparison against the exposed development pool. Every new-seed official candidate then needs its own GUI and reset admission under frozen code; the common model screenshot/action contract and hidden-data boundary must also be bound before researcher results. A change of seed or PDF appearance alone would not satisfy the causal-template requirement.

After this QA run, the final candidate ID scheme was strengthened to include a private-seed namespace. The 100 QA receipts used the earlier, untagged `HID` IDs; their positive/negative evidence supports the business rules, not the later package bytes. The tagged scheme requires its own GUI spot checks and all 100 new-seed task admissions before publication claims.
