# GitLab pre-result control resolution plan — 2026-09-27

At the [75-ID GUI-control checkpoint](gitlab-scoped-gui-progress-75-2026-09-27.md), **71/100** original final candidates had passed their evaluator-private positive / plausible negative / repeated positive trios. Four attempted IDs remained failed: three infrastructure-invalid controls and one deterministic **1/0/0** trio whose repeated positive missed persisted due-date readback. All four are retained in the mode-0600 append-only, hash-chained private ledger and the per-ID index. They are development qualification outcomes, **not model failures or official final admissions**. No model calls or researcher campaigns have occurred.

1. Finish the untouched candidate queue in bounded serial batches. At each boundary, reconcile the private index and failure ledger, verify the frozen business baseline, restore the pre-existing demo with its original identity, and publish only aggregate counts. A later failure expands the failed denominator; it must not be hidden by this plan.
2. Before any model campaign or final-set freeze, copy the completed private index as a checkpoint and audit every failed ID against its first-attempt receipt and ledger entry. Pin the operator revision and confirm its assignee and due-date save fixes on separate training-partition GUI controls. The known four failures must remain visible in all subsequent aggregate reporting.
3. Permit **at most one explicitly labeled requalification attempt per failed ID** on a fresh clone. For each, require a complete 1/0/1 GUI trio, independent saved-state and no-regression readback, and three verified cold resets. Check the exact eligible failed-ID set privately before a bounded `--retry-failed` sweep; do not rely on task ordering if later failures joined the queue. Append any second failure to the same ledger. A later pass changes the ID's latest qualification status but does not erase the first failure or turn it into a model outcome.
4. If an ID fails requalification or the control remains unstable, stop retries. Either leave GitLab below 100 qualified IDs, or quarantine its **entire correlated five-task project source family** before model execution. A replacement must use a new disjoint advisory/project family with private identities and gold, satisfy the train/selection/final separation and scoped ACLs, receive a new frozen GitLab baseline, and pass all five per-ID GUI trios and cold resets. Preserve the retired family's private records and publish aggregate replacement and failure counts. Replacing only a convenient failed variant is not allowed.

Only after all active 100 IDs pass the per-ID controls and the global preregistration, hidden-evaluator, actor-contract, and campaign gates pass may official admission be reconsidered. No source substitution may be chosen from model outcomes; this whole process is **pre-result**.
