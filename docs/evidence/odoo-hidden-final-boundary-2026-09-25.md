# Odoo hidden final boundary before task admission

**Status, 2026-09-25:** A new evaluator-private seed was generated only after the causal-rule QA was complete. The study now has three fresh, independently checkpointed Odoo worlds: 20 train identities, 20 selection identities and 100 `official_hidden` **candidates**. The existing v0.6 split validator accepts the combined task-package manifest. **Official final tasks admitted: 0/100.** No researcher or model has attempted this new pool.

The [boundary audit](odoo-hidden-final-boundary-2026-09-25.json) compared the new candidates against 14 exposed development pools and two older Odoo physical-filestore manifests, including the original four-family worker and earlier archived attempts. Task IDs, partners, SKUs, salespeople, source-document byte hashes, instance groups and causal template groups have zero overlap with those exposed pools. It also compared two evaluator-private QA workers: task IDs, entities, source bytes and instances are disjoint there as well. Those private QA workers intentionally exercised the same **four new business-rule templates** before final generation, so the audit explicitly reports four template-group overlaps with evaluator-owned QA. Their prompts, source values and gold were not placed in researcher contexts or public artifacts. The paper must not claim that the benchmark designers never tested these rules; the defensible boundary is that researchers/models see train and selection data, while evaluator-owned QA and hidden final data stay separate.

The new Purchase rule requires choosing a current supplier revision and correcting vendor reference. Inventory uses a demand-variability service-level calculation. Sales additionally changes quotation expiration from a customer deadline. CRM additionally replaces unverified intake email and phone. These alter business decisions or required Odoo actions relative to the exposed development-calibration set; changing the seed or PDF appearance alone was not treated as a new template. All business data and source documents for these four operational workflows are synthetic; the shared SEC historical excerpt remains reference-only and is not task gold.

This is an **identity and source-boundary audit**, not per-task qualification. Before any official final denominator is frozen, each of the 100 new-seed cases still needs native-GUI source readback, known-answer positive, wrong-object negative, independent saved-state/no-regression verification and exact cold database-and-filestore reset under the pinned worker lease. The 20 train and 20 selection cases also need their own gates. The common screenshot/action contract, evaluator-only gold and model-run budgets must then be bound without revealing hidden task contents.
