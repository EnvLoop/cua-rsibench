# Offline post-Enter settling candidate from public-train Desktop GUI evidence

The [read-only replay](native-wdi-v066-post-enter-train-offline-2026-09-29.json) opens only the original public-train Calc GUI positive, bound by its previously published receipt and source hashes. That train task belongs to a source-disjoint WDI training split. No selection or final task screenshot enters this candidate analysis. The source-bound train result independently verified its actor-saved spreadsheet; the present replay examines only the frame transitions after two Enter actions.

Both train transitions have the same recorded sequence. The first fresh observation (A) differed materially from its direct predispatch capture (B), and the original runner declined to dispatch. It requested a one-second no-action wait, then obtained a new observation and predispatch capture (B, B). In each case the three B application-image digests match exactly; the A→B difference is not a one-column caret blink. The saved frame bytes match their private receipt hashes, and the next action was normalized to the fresh observation frame. This is **two** supporting train events, not a measured distribution of stabilization times: the receipt records the runner's requested one-second sleep but not exact per-frame capture timestamps.

The additive [candidate policy](../../native_desktop_factory/post_enter_settle_candidate_v1.py) is deliberately offline-only. It recognizes exactly one material A→B transition after a prior Enter, one declared 1,000 ms no-action wait, and B→B across a **fresh** observation and exact predispatch capture. It returns no executable action and requires action generation from the fresh observation. A changed third state, another predispatch change, a caret-only difference, an unrelated key, an invalid frame, or a different wait declaration is rejected. Five portable adversarial tests pass. Existing frozen v4e source, permit, and one-shot intent are untouched; this candidate has no E2B or model entry point and carries no dispatch authority.

The evidence is **insufficient for a new paid final attempt**. Only two successful Calc Enter transitions are available. The retained Writer training positive has no Enter, and there are no live task-bound selection GUI trajectories. The raw train receipts do not measure a wall-clock stabilization distribution or show a persistent material third state, modal dialog, or never-stable transition under this candidate. Include successful saved-artifact controls, target-near negatives, cold reset, and adversarial modal/oscillating cases.
