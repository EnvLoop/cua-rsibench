One evaluator-private workbook was tested in the dedicated benchmark Microsoft account using **three distinct Excel for the web cloud items**. An operator repaired the positive copy, left exactly one target formula unrepaired in a near-miss copy, and opened a third clean actor copy without edits. Each cloud item was downloaded as an original-format `.xlsx`. The [aggregate receipt](sec-private-excel-web-development-triad-2026-09-27.json) publishes hashes of the three cloud item URLs and downloaded files; the URLs, source issuer, task text, formulas, answers and account identifiers stay evaluator-held.

Independent saved-OOXML readback of the **downloaded bytes** passed the positive copy across **57 formula targets** and two evaluator-side source/scenario perturbation profiles. Sheet order, tables, structure and unrelated cells remained intact. The near-miss download was rejected for the one unrepaired formula and its dependents under the baseline and both perturbations; it had no source, structure or collateral-regression errors. The positive and near-miss downloads differ at exactly one target formula. The third download failed the unsolved-task oracle as expected, while its formulas, source inputs, unrelated cells, sheets and tables matched the original actor seed semantically, confirming a clean fresh-copy reset. An evaluator-held receipt binds the three cloud item identities, byte hashes, verifier results and reset comparison.

This establishes **one original-software development control triad**, with one positive, one discriminating near miss and one reset. The positive repair was performed manually for transport and verifier calibration; **no model attempt** is scored.
