# PowerPoint web: quarantined four-edit development control

**Status, 2026-09-27:** one original four-edit candidate was used for evaluator-side Office compatibility and verifier development **before** any researcher campaign or official final outcome. Its entire four-task WDI country family is now quarantined. The original final inventory consequently has **96 remaining candidates and four replacement tasks pending**; official PowerPoint-web final admissions remain **0/100**. The [aggregate receipt](ppt-wdi-powerpoint-web-four-edit-development-2026-09-27.json) publishes hashes and counts, while the task identity, country, target text, cloud item URLs, and downloaded files remain evaluator-private.

The seven-slide source contains a real WDI observation table, a native chart with an embedded Excel workbook, and benchmark-authored analyst statements and committee rules. All 100 original candidate source decks passed offline native-chart preflight. An initial staging pass made 400 raw role copies, but the development control showed why raw positive/negative copies must not become the frozen attempt state: Office rewrites target-region package data on its first save. The revised staging process creates only the raw baseline copy and reserves the other role names until the evaluator can materialize them from a frozen Office-saved baseline. Staging is **not** GUI admission. For this development control, the evaluator uploaded the original source to PowerPoint for the web, made reversible trusted edits in **all four target regions** (summary shape, calculation table cell, interpretation shape, decision shape), restored their original content, and downloaded the Office-saved baseline. Visible text in all seven slides, the embedded workbook bytes, and the chart's numerical/label content still matched the original source. The evaluator then uploaded three **byte-identical copies of that normalized baseline** for separate positive, near-miss, and fresh-reset attempts.

Two earlier near-miss **pre-action controller failures** inserted a blank slide while keyboard `Enter` was used on PowerPoint thumbnails. Both eight-slide downloaded artifacts and private failure receipts were preserved. They were not scored as model failures and were not silently retried. The valid control used pointer-click slide navigation, checked the complete seven-slide state and visible target before every edit, and left the summary deliberately unresolved. The downloaded positive was rendered to seven-page private PDF inspection material and every page was visually reviewed; the near-miss first page was compared separately.

The [independent OOXML verifier](../../ppt_wdi_factory/verify.py) freezes the Office-saved baseline and permits only observed editor bookkeeping in targeted regions: target text-run segmentation and limited default-font/style fallback, a table's opaque modification ID and invisible paragraph-end size, the chart's opaque series UUID, and narrowly defined Office metadata. It still compares non-default target styling, non-target shapes/tables, chart values, workbook bytes, relationships, and package members. A changed chart value or non-target slide text remains a preservation failure. The [private control auditor](../../tools/audit_ppt_wdi_web_final_development_v1.py) additionally binds the distinct cloud item URL hashes, downloaded hashes, source-family quarantine, and the two invalid-attempt receipts.

This result establishes that a complex original task can be edited and independently checked in the **real PowerPoint web application**. It does not justify including the development family in a hidden final exam or claiming model performance.
