# Hard SaaS task controls and per-task admission gate

The [source-pinned two-cell matrix](saas-100-candidate-admission-2026-09-25.md) still contains **20 selection and 100 provisional final candidates per SaaS application**, with zero sealed official final identities. The [control assessor](../../tools/assess_saas_control_receipts_v1.py) reads private task-specific runner results, recomputes pinned source identity and task-record hashes, and fails closed on incorrect official score, missing independent persisted-state readback, raw/sanitized trace disagreement, or reset mismatch. A public upstream task cannot be promoted into a hidden final instance by a local development receipt. The [field-limited assessment](saas-hard-control-assessment-2026-09-25.json) reports one qualified development workflow and one rejected, unscored attempt.

| Cell and source task | Actual GUI/state control | Admission outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GitLab hard task 590 | Fresh disposable GitLab CE clone; official correct/near-miss/correct scores **1.0/0.0/1.0**; PostgreSQL target readback and reset after every case | One **development workflow qualified** on a minimal synthetic project; not an original populated seed or official final task |
| Magento hard task 499 | First clone persisted the requested shipment/track, but raw versus sanitized HAR evaluation disagreed; full-content HAR retry in a fresh clone timed out without a saved shipment while local search failed | **Rejected, unscored**; no valid positive/negative/reset pair |

The exact GitLab evidence is in the [fresh-clone milestone receipt](gitlab-hard-milestone-repeat-2026-09-25.md). The Magento failure modes are kept separate in the [shipment rejection receipt](magento-hard-shipment-rejection-2026-09-25.md). Neither attempt used a model or paid provider. After the probes, both disposable clones were removed; the original GitLab demo returned healthy with the same identity, image, ports, and volume destinations, while the shared Magento smoke and Odoo containers remained running.

The assessor currently has task-specific adapters for these two controls. To advance toward 100 admitted tasks per cell, each candidate needs a runner that generates an independently checked native-GUI positive and plausible negative, saved-state or answer/destination oracle, unrelated-state preservation, and a fresh exact reset. A stable original populated seed or evaluator-owned equivalent and unpublished final variants remain separate gates. The assessment deliberately keeps official-final count at **zero**.
