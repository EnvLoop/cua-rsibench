The [source receipt](office-ppt-transfer-expansion-source-2026-09-29.json) and [independent read-only source audit](office-ppt-transfer-expansion-source-audit-2026-09-29.json) bind the selected ZIPs, extracts, retrieval date, response headers and source hashes without publishing country identities. It added six source families to each of the ten four-target causal workflows, yielding eight distinct sources per workflow. The original study's train, selection and final-candidate anchors were unchanged. All 80 passed their direct-file controls. No pilot row or source package was replaced after observing an outcome.

Ten representative decks, one per workflow from a newly captured source family, were exported to 70 private PDF pages with LibreOffice Impress. We visually reviewed both full contact sheets and three full-resolution pages; no clipping or missing content was found in this representative sample. The private PDFs, images and review receipt are hashed in the artifact audit.
