# Native desktop E2B control-plane interruption and recovery

Checked 2026-09-25. During a bounded calibration sweep of private WDI-derived LibreOffice final candidates, E2B began returning consecutive `ConnectError` failures **before artifact staging or GUI action**. The sweep was interrupted rather than counting them as model/task failures or repeatedly creating sandboxes. The original run journal and all attempt receipts remain in ignored evaluator storage. The [field-limited public audit](native-wdi-e2b-interruption-2026-09-25.json) binds their hashes without disclosing private task IDs, gold, provider credentials, or raw sandbox IDs.

At interruption, 32 new attempt directories existed. Sixteen held `ConnectError` evidence, including 13 without an acknowledged sandbox ID and two with an inconclusive post-kill check. All 16 had **zero actor actions, no staged task artifact, and no saved result**. Their potential server-side 300-second leases were allowed to expire, followed by a 90-second grace interval. A provider active-sandbox query then matched **zero** known hashes from this run. A separate, bounded 120-second E2B Desktop health probe successfully created a sandbox, checked the provider-reported template identity and 8-vCPU/8-GiB resource class, verified LibreOffice 7.3.7.2, and confirmed termination. Only after those checks were the 16 original attempt directories moved to a hash-bound infrastructure-invalid archive; one controlled retry per archived attempt is permitted, with the original evidence intact.

The pre-retry admission snapshot showed **12/100** final candidates passing the task-specific GUI trio, **88/100** awaiting complete controls, and **zero canonical invalid receipts** after the infrastructure-invalid archive. These are engineering calibrations, not student/model outcomes. The 32 started attempt leases correspond to a conservative **$2.6667** reserve at the subsequently adopted $1/hour planning cap; the original large run had a $25 planned ceiling, which is not an incurred charge. [E2B prices compute by the second](https://e2b.dev/pricing), but the SDK receipts do not provide a per-run invoice. Actual billed dollars, credits, discounts, and any plan fees remain **unknown**. No score is assigned to any interrupted task; official full-study final admissions and model results remain **zero**.
