# Bounded PowerPoint source replacement after seven quarantines

The [aggregate commitment](ppt-wdi-future-reserve-queue-2026-09-27.json) freezes a private, alphabetically ordered list of ten unused WDI country source codes and a maximum of **three further four-task family replacements**. This rule was added after seven evaluator-only source families had been quarantined and **before any hidden model outcome or official final admission**. The identities remain private to protect the proposed final pool; the queue SHA-256 is public so future replacements can be audited against today's order.

For any new replacement the evaluator must take the first unused country in that order whose official World Bank CSV contains all five pinned indicators for every year 2019–2024. Missing or invalid sources are retained as rejected candidates, rather than skipped silently. Each accepted replacement still needs a fresh private seed, exact workflow/target-location balance, 140-deck offline controls and individual original-software GUI qualification. A replacement is never counted as an official task merely because the pool is refilled.

If total quarantined source families would exceed **ten**, the PowerPoint cell fails its current pre-campaign gate. The team must publish a new study registration and rebuild the cell before model training or full-matrix scoring, rather than continuing to choose easier sources until 100 happen to pass. Seven prior quarantines are disclosed as attrition and cannot be erased from the final technical report. This cap does not relax the existing one-retry, chart-preservation, independent-verifier or source-isolation rules.
