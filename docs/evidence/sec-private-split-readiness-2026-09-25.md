# Private Excel split readiness: blocked, not a 100-task result

The [executable structural screen](../../sec_excel_factory/screen_private_split.py) currently reports [zero registered evaluator-held cases](sec-private-split-readiness-2026-09-25.json): **0 train / 0 selection / 0 final**. Thus the official Microsoft Excel for the web final admission count remains **0/100**. The earlier 20/20/100 integrated workbooks are offline *candidates* sharing Apple/Microsoft issuer identities and one final calculation template; they are not a sealed, source-independent exam. The ten structural workflows built since then are also **public development controls**, not hidden finals.

The preregistered split requires 20 train, 20 selection and 100 final cases; at most five cases per issuer; at least 20 final issuer families and 28 private issuer families in total; and at least ten final semantic workflow families. Issuer, original accession and semantic-template families must not cross train/selection/final splits. A private source issuer or template must not reuse any of the 28 issuer identities or ten structural workflows already exposed in public development material. Duplicate actor/reference artifact hashes and repeated instances of the same issuer, semantic template and original filing are rejected. Each private case needs at least three independently verified canonical filing facts; the ten public two-fact anchors do not qualify for complex workflows merely by having an SEC index and two official-view values.

With the current registry, the **minimum new source inventory is 28 additional issuer families** that have independently checked task-relevant facts: at least four train, four selection and 20 final issuer families at five tasks each. The final set also needs **ten newly authored and independently reviewed workflow graphs**, since all ten existing graphs are public. The two-fact anchors and proxy-only facts cannot be promoted by relabeling them. A new private manifest and its source excerpts, actor/reference files, oracle details and perturbation controls must stay evaluator-held; publish aggregate commitments and methods, not hidden gold.

The screen checks structural claims and exact manifest fields. A passing synthetic test plan demonstrates that the screen accepts a well-formed allocation, **not** that such financial cases exist or are authentic. An actual case requires source-fact review against its original filing, independent saved-OOXML positive and negative controls, counterfactual/no-regression checks, and a clean copy opened, saved, downloaded, read back and reset in the real Microsoft Excel web surface. Each of the 100 final IDs needs those controls before admission. The current failed download/readback transport cannot be bypassed by counting local `.xlsx` files or a cloud URL.

Run the structural screen against an evaluator-held manifest only after those candidate cases exist:

```bash
python3 sec_excel_factory/screen_private_split.py \
  --private-manifest work/private-excel/exam-manifest.json \
  --public-out work/private-excel/structural-screen.json
```

The private manifest must be an object with a `tasks` array. Each row carries `task_id`, `split`, `issuer_cik`, `original_10k_accession`, `source_excerpt_sha256`, `source_provenance_tier` equal to `independently_verified_exact_facts`, `verified_canonical_fact_count`, `template_family`, `template_semantic_sha256`, `actor_sha256` and `reference_sha256`. This screen is a **necessary allocation gate**, not an authenticity verifier or an official benchmark score; its output always reports zero official Excel-web admissions. Keep real per-case receipts under the evaluator, then publish only audited aggregate counts.
