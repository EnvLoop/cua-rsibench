# Native WDI Desktop v0.6.6 real GUI smoke — public training fixture only

The [aggregate receipt](native-wdi-v066-train-primitive-live-2026-09-27.json) independently rechecks five private E2B Desktop attempts against a committed, public Impress **training** fixture. No Tinker/model was called; no hidden final package, oracle, checkpoint, or score was touched. Each fresh guest passed the independently frozen scoped guest-content digest before document staging, and each sandbox was terminated.

The first attempt applied one screenshot-bound GUI click. A slide transition then changed the physical frame between observation and dispatch. The adapter **rejected** that action as `PhysicalFrameDrift`, preserved the failure receipt, and killed the sandbox. A bounded repair reobserved the same step without weakening the current-frame rule. The second attempt applied **10/10** GUI actions including native double-click and focused insertion; the saved PPTX passed the independent train verifier. The third applied **16/16** actions and additionally executed `Control+F`, `Control+H`, `Control+End`, and `Shift+End` through the proposed v0.6.6 dispatcher; its saved PPTX also passed. Every applied step records the observation screenshot hash, randomized frame-ID hash, immediate pre-dispatch screenshot hash, validated action digest, and dispatch result. The successful attempts retained raw observation screenshots and hashes of the pre-dispatch frames, but **not the raw pre-dispatch screenshots**.

The fourth attempt added raw private pre-dispatch screenshot retention, then correctly stopped after a `Control+H` dialog transition exceeded its bounded fresh-frame retry limit at step 13. The fifth increased the retry ceiling and allowed the dialog to settle, but an E2B `ReadError` interrupted it after three applied GUI actions. Both failures remain separate infrastructure/timing evidence, and neither is a model failure or scored task. Their already applied actions retained raw observation and pre-dispatch screenshots; no complete positive with raw pre-dispatch frames was obtained before the development lane reached its cap.

Official Desktop admissions and final model results remain **zero**.
