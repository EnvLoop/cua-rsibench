# Odoo global business-record no-regression control

**Development control, 2026-09-25; not an official final task or model result.** The first four-family Odoo evaluator compared only benchmark-origin Purchase and Sales records, benchmark CRM opportunities, replenishment rules, related partners/products and source attachments. A new unrelated business record could escape those scoped row checks. This is a real false-acceptance risk for a task that otherwise reaches the expected target values.

The revised read-only PostgreSQL snapshot also records identity sets for 15 global business groups: Purchase orders and lines; Sales orders and lines; stock pickings, moves and move lines; accounting entries and lines; CRM leads; partners; users; products; replenishment rules; and business-attached files. These identity sets reject insertion or deletion of unrelated business records while allowing normal Odoo web-asset caches and chatter activity. The evaluator still scopes **field-value** equality to the benchmark entities and protected source documents; it does not claim complete equality across every Odoo table.

In a disposable local worker, a role-scoped browser actor read the Product Purchase planning note, changed the matching WH/Stock rule's minimum and maximum through the native GUI, and the independent evaluator accepted the persisted target with reward 1. The same actor then created an unrelated Product through Odoo's native GUI. With the original pre-action snapshot held fixed, the independent evaluator returned reward 0 and the specific code `global_business_record_identity_changed`. The [machine-readable control](odoo-global-no-regression-development-2026-09-25.json) reports the result without exposing the private task package or credentials.

This control prompted a **new** database-and-filestore checkpoint and a fresh per-ID gate. Earlier GUI receipts were invalidated as development QA; they cannot be counted as official admission under the stronger verifier.
