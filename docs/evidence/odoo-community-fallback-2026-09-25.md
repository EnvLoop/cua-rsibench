# Odoo Community original-software fallback: two-workflow development gate

**Status, 2026-09-25:** Original Odoo Community 18.0 GUI deployed locally; two development workflows and a database/filestore reset protocol controlled. **Official final tasks: 0/100. Researcher campaigns: 0. Model results: none.** This is a candidate substitute for a currently inaccessible ServiceNow WorkArena cell, not a WorkArena result or an automatic change to a preregistered matrix.

## What was actually deployed

- `odoo:18.0-20260908` at OCI index digest `sha256:478065867b945579649373aa4cca3b56f7b74daf0522e5fdf85e7d1262e11d39`, Community modules `contacts`, `crm`, `sale_management`, `purchase`, and `stock`; `postgres:15` at index digest `sha256:724292da1f2e50bdccfc3302ce75bbba7f4a6076701b588cc795fcac65683550`.
- Odoo login returned HTTP 200 on loopback. The native Purchase and Inventory views exposed seeded RFQs, product Purchase notes, and Replenishment rules. A browser read of `/web/database/manager` showed the *database manager has been disabled* banner after `--no-database-list`; HTTP 200 alone was not taken as evidence of enabled management.
- Seeded 24 synthetic suppliers, 48 synthetic goods SKUs, 120 synthetic three-line RFQs, 120 synthetic supplier-confirmation PDFs, 20 synthetic product planning notes, and 20 warehouse replenishment rules. The RFQ set has **eight error patterns with 15 cases each**. Selection/scale overlap is exactly **0 RFQ IDs, 20 vendors, 36 SKUs, and all eight fault patterns**, so the nominal 20/100 partition is **not leak-safe**. The train-only generator creates 40 undeployed candidates with zero ID/vendor/SKU overlap against both partitions; it is not a training run.
- Separately attached the existing 112-row unmodified public SEC companyfacts excerpt (`sha256:df9f56387b2c30bc521d8a4eb13c6e437a065d3b921ed4ae3a5fab1e1f20f64a`). A read of the actual Odoo filestore file produced the same SHA-256 as the published excerpt. It is reference-only. All Odoo prices, quantities, dates, vendor relationships, PDF confirmations, and demand notes are synthetic.

## Visible GUI and independent verifier controls

The evaluator reads PostgreSQL through `bench_verify`, which has `SELECT` and lacks `UPDATE` on the observed tables. No browser text or Odoo XML-RPC response determines reward. The baseline snapshot covers all 120 RFQs, all 360 lines, 121 attachments (including the SEC reference), 24 vendors, 48 products with planning notes, and 20 replenishment rules.

1. **Purchase, multi-line positive:** In the real Odoo GUI, a human development operator opened `ELPO-0005`, previewed its supplier PDF in Odoo's PDF.js viewer, and read confirmed prices of `$45.50` and `$54.00` for two different SKUs. The RFQ showed `$55.00` and `$63.50`. After changing both through the RFQ editor, saving, and reloading, the independent SQL evaluator returned `reward=1.0`.
2. **Purchase, wrong-object negative:** The operator then changed a price on a separate `ELPO-0001` through the GUI. Scoring `ELPO-0005` fell to `reward=0.0`, `unrelated_order_line_changed`. This proves the tested guard rejects at least that extra side effect; it does not prove all possible regressions are covered.
3. **Inventory, cross-screen positive:** In the product Purchase tab for `EL-SCANNER-01`, the GUI showed weekly demand `5, 10, 15, 9`, seven-day lead, safety stock `3`, and case pack `4`. The stated formula yields minimum `13` and maximum `21`. The Inventory/Replenishment GUI initially showed `15/19`; the operator saved `13/21`, reloaded, and the independent SQL evaluator returned `reward=1.0`.
4. **Inventory, wrong-rule negative:** A GUI change to the neighboring `EL-SCANNER-02` minimum caused the `ELRP-0001` score to fall to `reward=0.0`, `unrelated_replenishment_rule_changed`.
5. **Different supplier and fault pattern:** A fresh reset preceded `ELPO-0004` for Pinecrest Logistics. The attached PDF required one SKU line to change from quantity `24` and unit price `$51.25` to `18` and `$43.00`. Both fields persisted through a GUI save/reload; read-only SQL scored `1.0`. Another full reset returned it to `0.0`.
6. **Different Inventory stratum:** A fresh reset preceded `ELRP-0002` for `EL-SCANNER-02`. Its Purchase note showed demand `8, 13, 7, 12`, a 14-day lead, safety stock `4`, and case pack `6`. The GUI rule changed from `27/33` to calculated `24/36`; SQL scored `1.0`, then reset restored a `0.0` baseline.
7. **Reset:** After each GUI positive/negative sequence, the instance was restored from its exact custom PostgreSQL dump and physical filestore archive. The current two-family checkpoint contains **1,346 filestore files**. After a reset, the evaluator-scope database snapshot matched its frozen baseline, and the complete physical filestore file-path/SHA-256 manifest matched **before** Odoo restarted. All four development cases scored `0.0` again at baseline. A separate test added a throwaway attachment; reset removed that extra physical file and restored the same manifest.

The supplier confirmation was first prototyped as `.txt`; Odoo listed it but showed a blank inline preview. The fixture was changed to a one-page PDF, rendered and visually checked locally, then its Odoo PDF.js preview was inspected in the native UI. The earlier TXT-stage controls are excluded from the PDF-stage GUI evidence.

## Admission boundary

These are **two** implemented operational source families: supplier-confirmation PDFs for RFQ reconciliation and product Purchase planning notes for Inventory replenishment. The SEC excerpt is one historical public reference source, not a third operational workflow. Two GUI-positive cases per family and one GUI wrong-object case per family were observed; no per-case 120/20 sweep, model execution, hidden gold sealing, or isolated multi-worker run has been completed. No candidate is admitted to an official 100-task final set.

The current RFQ task asks for three-line reconciliation with one to three mismatches; its eight perturbation patterns do not create eight independent task families. To qualify a publication-scale enterprise cell, the [receipt, invoice, and permissions contracts](../../enterprise_fallback/odoo18/README.md#next-workflow-contracts) must be implemented and tested, an entity-disjoint train/selection/final split and its template-generalization endpoint must be preregistered and frozen, and every selected final task must pass a fresh GUI save/readback/reset plus negative controls. The same **Odoo Community** software may then replace ServiceNow only through an explicit preregistered protocol revision made before official model outcomes.

An **undeployed** `proposed_clustered_split(world_seed)` generator repairs the entity overlap on paper: 40 train cases across 8 vendor clusters, 20 selection across 4, and 100 evaluation candidates across 20; each cluster has five cases and SKU namespaces are also split-disjoint. Tests confirm zero ID/vendor/SKU overlap. It keeps all eight RFQ fault patterns in each split, so it supports only a declared *within-template* generalization endpoint. No generated evaluation candidate was seeded, hidden, or GUI-admitted in this run.

## Rights and limits

The tested software is the original [Odoo Community LGPLv3 source](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/blob/18.0/LICENSE) in an [official Odoo container](https://hub.docker.com/_/odoo), backed by [PostgreSQL](https://www.postgresql.org/about/licence/). The original [SEC XBRL companyfacts API](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces) supplies public historical numeric observations. No ServiceNow/WorkArena gated task content, personal account, or production data was copied into this fixture. The benchmark does not claim that full issuer filings are freely licensed for wholesale republication. This local prototype has no proven E2B fleet orchestration, concurrent-worker isolation, accounting workflow, or 100-task GUI admission.
