# Full-study paid-dispatch budget ledger (offline controller v1)

The [pre-campaign plan](FULL_STUDY_PRE_CAMPAIGN.md) declares 24 equal researcher caps and six shared-base final-evaluation reserves. `src/cursibench/full_study_budget_v1.py` turns those declarations into a private append-only dollar ledger. It is **not** a payment API, invoice, or permission to dispatch an unqualified cell.

Before an external request, the controller records a unique attempt ID, owner cell/researcher, service category, exact-work SHA-256, and a worst-case dollar reservation. Supported categories are Tinker, researcher inference, teacher rollout, E2B, storage/application services, and selected final evaluation; shared-base finals have their own owners. The ledger refuses category, per-campaign, and global over-reservation. It then records the hash of the exact outgoing request as `dispatch_started` **before** sending it. If the provider result is uncertain, the full reservation stays charged and the same work cannot be sent under a new attempt ID. A provider-usage receipt settles a bounded attempt; a trusted no-charge receipt cancels it and releases the reserve. If the provider actually charges more than the reserved envelope, the ledger records the overrun rather than hiding it and freezes all new paid dispatch. The ledger rejects a changed replay, checks its hash chain on every operation, writes with `fsync`, and restricts the file to the local owner.

The dollar amounts support nine decimal places so small published-rate token subtotals such as the [train-only Qwen smoke](evidence/magento-price777-tinker-billing-reconciliation-2026-09-25.md) can be reconciled without rounding away costs. Token, call, sandbox-hour, wall-time, and candidate-count caps still need their separate runtime counters and provider receipts. A local hash or caller-supplied no-charge SHA-256 cannot itself prove a provider actually charged nothing; independent cost review remains required. The existing test fixture exercises idempotency, an uncertain transport outcome, same-work retry prevention, a provider settlement, cap rejection, no-charge cancellation, and tamper detection. It makes **no** paid call.

`tools/full_study_budget_v1.py` exposes `snapshot`, `reserve`, `dispatch-started`, `uncertain`, `settle`, and `cancel-no-charge` commands. Every invocation rebuilds the pre-campaign plan from its source manifest before opening the ledger; it does not trust a caller-supplied plan JSON. The ledger belongs under ignored private `work/` and its exact request, usage, and no-charge proof hashes must be archived alongside the application and provider receipts.

There are currently **zero** qualified real pre-campaign plans and **zero** full-study campaigns. The one-step development Tinker smoke is outside this future 24-campaign ledger. A paid dispatcher may use this controller only after the real 600-task pre-campaign freeze has passed and its declared balances and service caps have been checked against current provider access.
