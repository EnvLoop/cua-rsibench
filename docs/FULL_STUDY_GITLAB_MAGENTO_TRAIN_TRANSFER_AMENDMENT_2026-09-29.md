# GitLab and Magento train-only transfer amendment, 2026-09-29

**Status: offline source amendment, before model evaluation.** The [six-cell
difficulty audit](evidence/full-study-six-cell-difficulty-transfer-audit-2026-09-29.md)
found that the original GitLab training tasks teach only isolated issue,
milestone, and membership edits, while the 100 candidate final tasks require
five composite workflows. Magento's original training task edits one SKU at a
stated price, while its candidate final tasks calculate and save five SKU
prices under four different policies. A student might learn the UI primitives
without learning those compositions. No Qwen floor, ceiling, or LoRA effect has
been measured from this source diagnosis.

The separate [GitLab builder](../gitlab_world/train_transfer_analogues.py)
stages two supplemental **train-only** candidates for each of five workflow
skills, using two distinct original training projects per skill. The staged
prompts rephrase the objective and still require cross-record issue triage,
two-issue milestone coordination, choosing between two open MRs, an access
handoff, or coordinated CI/runbook edits. Each candidate declares its
source facts, target state fields, a concrete plausible wrong action, and
the unrelated records or files that must be preserved. The original 20 train,
20 selection, and 100 candidate final tasks are untouched. The builder
recomputes the original partition audit and checks CVE, vendor, project,
asset, principal, and source-family separation from selection and final.

The separate [Magento builder](../magento_catalog_factory/train_transfer_analogues.py)
stages two supplemental **train-only** candidates for each of the four final
arithmetic skills, using eight different original training parent products.
Each requires calculating and saving five child SKU prices. The offline
validator independently recomputes all five prices from quote facts, verifies
the quote body contains only source facts, and defines a wrong-policy
one-SKU near miss plus unchanged catalog scope. The actual training inventory
has seven parents with at least seven children and another nine with six;
the selected eight therefore have seven cases with two untouched same-parent
comparators and one with one. All eight still have five target SKUs. The
train-only stock quotes include one SKU exactly at the inclusive threshold
and another just below it; the parity quotes include a non-nickel raw price
that requires upward rounding. These source-fact stresses do not change the
original final plan. The
builder rejects any selection/final parent, child, SKU, or source-family
overlap and any shortage of eligible train parents. The original Magento
candidate plan and all final identities remain byte-for-byte unchanged.

The private staged packages, including all task IDs, CVEs, product names,
instructions, source allocations, quote bytes, and target prices, remain in
ignored owner-only `work/` storage. Only field-limited aggregate receipts are
published: [GitLab](evidence/gitlab-train-transfer-two-per-skill-2026-09-29.json)
and [Magento](evidence/magento-train-transfer-two-per-policy-v2-2026-09-29.json).
Those receipts bind the original factory source, new builder source, and
original public aggregate. Their hashes are evidence of the **offline source
staging**, not evidence of authentic GUI completion or model improvement.
The [read-only reopening audit](evidence/gitlab-magento-train-transfer-reopen-2026-09-29.json)
revalidated all 18 exact private packages, both public receipts, source pins,
and owner-only private file modes without publishing private task content.
An earlier Magento-only source draft remains privately retained and is marked
superseded by the [amendment log](evidence/magento-train-transfer-v1-supersession-2026-09-29.json)
because the adopted v2 adds exact stock-threshold and non-nickel parity
rounding stress before any GUI or model run.

Before adding these tasks to an SFT campaign, each candidate still needs a
fresh original-software positive, plausible wrong-action negative, and cold
reset; an independent persisted-state scorer must check exact target and
no-regression state. Teacher demonstrations must capture valid current-frame
actions and saved artifacts. The task-disjoint selection set remains the
original 20 and does not yet cover these composite skills. If this challenge
pool is adopted, all four researcher configurations must receive the same
pool and a new pre-campaign source/budget freeze. No task may be promoted
based on an official final outcome.

This first stage stops at **two cases per skill**. Scaling toward eight per
skill requires train-only difficulty and GUI-validity evidence. The five
existing GitLab training projects and sixteen Magento training parents with
six or more children cannot supply eight globally unique source families per
skill without a further separately frozen source expansion; repeated
train-source cases must be labeled as correlated, not counted as independent
source families. The official counts remain **0/600 final admissions, 0/24
campaigns, and 0/3,000 matched final outcomes**.
