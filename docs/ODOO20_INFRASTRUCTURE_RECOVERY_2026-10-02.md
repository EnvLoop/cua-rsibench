# Odoo20 infrastructure interruption and recovery

This amendment concerns the single-environment pilot. It does not admit tasks or results to the larger six-environment study. Sol 6 remains the researcher and GUI teacher; Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B remains the student.

## Observed interruption

Tinker returned HTTP 402 during both baseline selection and real LoRA training. An independent billing-gated capabilities read confirmed that access remained blocked. The baseline retained three completed, independently scored selection cases. The fourth attempt completed 44 model samples before a subsequent request timed out with uncertain completion. It has no saved model score or completed actor lifecycle and is classified as an infrastructure exclusion. Outer cleanup independently restored its database, filestore and services.

Training completed 19 optimizer steps out of the fixed 192-step schedule. The next forward/backward request became uncertain while billing was paused. Closing the client subsequently produced a secondary holder-closed error. No sampler checkpoint was saved. These partial operations are not a trained model or a measured improvement, and unknown billing remains null.

## Selection continuation

The additive V4 orchestration reopens the three completed cases using the original saved verifier, native source, sampler setup, clocks and reset receipts. It preserves their original provenance and mirrors all 5,827 retained evidence files byte-for-byte. It excludes the interrupted fourth attempt without assigning a score.

Continuation requires an actual successful post-interruption capabilities response for the same student model, owned client close and a hash-bound root review. A one-use authority allows a fresh whole-task replacement for the interrupted fourth case and the 16 previously unattempted cases. It forbids replaying any request from the interrupted attempt. The three completed cases are imported and never dispatched again. Native actor, sampler, scorer, reset and per-task limits remain unchanged: 90 actions, 720 actor seconds and 1,200 complete lifecycle seconds.

The checkpoint lane must execute all 20 selection tasks afresh through the same orchestration source. Checkpoint promotion still requires matched task identities and per-task no-regression. Selection is frozen before any final model outcome. The final evaluator remains unchanged and requires 20 base and 20 checkpoint outcomes.

## Training recovery boundary

The interrupted request's server future identifier was not retained, and no weights or optimizer checkpoint was saved. The evidence does not establish an exact continuation point. No request identifier or optimizer sequence may be guessed. A fresh training attempt, if needed after access is restored, must explicitly reference the failed attempt and consumed dataset identity, retain the original 103 datums and fixed hyperparameters, and report the failed attempt separately. It must not silently bypass the consumed-dataset guard or describe partial training as the completed schedule.

New training instrumentation must record submitted future identifiers before waiting and surface billing faults promptly. Final reporting requires genuine receipts for the complete schedule, sampler save and readback, settled owned calls, matched final outcomes and visual PDF review. No publication of a training-effect result is authorized by this partial evidence.
