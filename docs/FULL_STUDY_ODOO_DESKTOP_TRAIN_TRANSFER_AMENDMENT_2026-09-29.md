## Admission and reporting boundary

The original anchors and hidden final identities are byte-for-byte unchanged. Neither new pool is part of the formal 20-task train count yet. All four researcher configurations must receive the same admitted supplemental pool under a new pre-campaign source and budget freeze. Each Odoo case still needs a fresh original-software positive, plausible wrong-action negative, independent persisted PostgreSQL/filestore readback, and cold reset. Each Desktop case needs fresh LibreOffice GUI positive/near-miss/reset evidence with saved OOXML, profile and guest isolation checks, and valid current-frame teacher actions before SFT admission. The existing 20 selection tasks remain shallower; selection transfer coverage needs its own pre-result amendment or an explicit extrapolation stratum.

This first stage is **two cases per workflow**. The [paired train-only difficulty screen](TRAIN_ONLY_DIFFICULTY_CALIBRATION_2026-09-27.md) requires eight valid base/reference pairs from at least two sources per represented workflow before a ceiling/floor statement. Full coverage at that threshold would require 32 diagnostic instances in each of these four-workflow cells. No paid diagnostic, campaign, or final model result is inferred from the staged packages. The official study remains **0/600 final admissions, 0/24 campaigns, and 0/3,000 matched final outcomes**.
