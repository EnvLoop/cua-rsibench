# Uniform pre-result policy amendment

The later human instruction authorizes spending without a dollar ceiling and asks for effective results. The old USD 500 Tinker ceiling is no longer the requested policy. This amendment must be frozen before any official results and applied identically to the shared base and four checkpoint slots. The native TRAIN diagnostics remain outside official study results, and no historical receipt or score is reclassified.

The new policy separates three facts. Performance is the independently verified saved artifact and fresh reset outcome. Inference status records whether the last model request completed, stayed uncertain, or completed too late for GUI application. Billing records authentic observed charges or remains null until reconciliation. Provider close acknowledgement proves closure of the owned service; it does not prove a missing response or an invoice.

For a budget-ended task, performance eligibility requires the actual saved artifact, unchanged independent scorer, distinct reset with original bytes, both owned guests terminated, proven no late GUI action, a hash-verified deadline RPC and bounded provider close acknowledgement. Earlier provider faults remain infrastructure-invalid. The adapter reads the score already established by the verifier; it never creates score zero from a timeout. Every uncertain paid intent remains retained, and no missing model result, output-token count or charge is fabricated.

The new unlimited-dollar ledger has no category, owner or global dollar ceiling. Nominal quotes remain accounting estimates and are not authorization limits. Authentic usage above an estimate is retained without freezing unrelated work. Unknown charges stay null, rather than becoming numeric zero or the reservation amount. Exact-request intent, hash chain, owner/category scope, duplicate-work prevention and no-charge evidence requirements remain enforced. Provider authentication, balance availability and actual provider faults are not assumed away.

The amendment retains 16 campaign hours, every existing compute/count limit, 90 actor actions, 720 actor seconds, 1,200-second leases, the six-cell/24-campaign matrix, 600 distinct official identities and 3,000 initial slot-task results. No time, compute, action, task, guard or scorer limit is relaxed. Direct human authorization and the zero-official-results review are separately hash-bound evidence. Changing any retained limit invalidates the amendment.

The first actionable source pieces are implemented separately from the live v21 namespace:

- `full_study_policy_amendment_v2` builds and validates the explicit pre-result amendment against the existing qualified plan and retained compute limits.
- `full_study_unlimited_budget_v2.StudyBudgetLedger` applies no dollar ceilings while preserving authentic append-only usage and unresolved calls.
- `scored_budget_outcome_policy_v22` reopens the actual v21 saved/scored/reset, clock, guest and shutdown artifacts and returns independent performance, inference and billing fields.
- `full_study_performance_coverage_v2` retains every paid intent and accepts only checked budget-ended performance evidence for an uncertain final sample. It never changes that sample to completed or settles its invoice.

The real native v21 TRAIN case was read-only checked by the adapter. Its journal has 23 request rows: 22 completed responses and one uncertain error. It applied 22 clicks, independently scored zero, restored a distinct guest from original bytes and acknowledged provider close. The retained paid set contains 26 intents and 25 result files; the missing uncertain model result stays missing. Cost and billed tokens remain null. Without an approved formal amendment binding, the diagnostic adapter reports performance coverage as ineligible for formal registration.

Legacy blockers have been located precisely. `full_study_matrix_v1` hardcodes Tinker USD 500 and requires equality in `_budget`; `full_study_pre_campaign_v1` copies category, owner and global ceilings. `full_study_budget_v1` rejects cap overruns and freezes further work when authentic usage exceeds a nominal reservation. Campaign SFT quote checks, shared-base separate reservation checks, final per-task reservations, results cap validation and paper wording also carry dollar ceilings.

Performance and billing are coupled elsewhere. The old paid coverage gate requires every Tinker task result to be completed. Campaign selection registration requires every paid intent in `paid_result`. Campaign freeze and the final gate require all calls settled or cancelled, with no uncertain usage. Shared-base reconciliation and verification require invoices and amounts within the old reservation. The final outcome parser requires a numeric cost and complete output-token counts. These interfaces must gain explicit amended schemas and bindings; simply replacing the ledger would not remove every ceiling or make the missing response known.

The next coordinated change is to freeze a new matrix/pre-campaign manifest and public pre-result witness containing the amendment hash, then wire the new ledger and performance coverage into campaign selection, shared-base verification, final dispatch and results/publication. Verified performance can be reported while inference completion or invoice completeness remains false. Complete financial publication must continue to disclose unknown costs. No legacy validator is silently bypassed, no live source file is changed, and no source-only adapter is an official registration or provider call.
