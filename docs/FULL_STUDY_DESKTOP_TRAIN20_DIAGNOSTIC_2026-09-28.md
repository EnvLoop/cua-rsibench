# Original WDI Desktop 20-Identity Training Diagnostic

This is an evaluator-controlled **training-data and effect diagnostic**, separate from the six-cell official study. The source is the original private WDI-derived LibreOffice 20/20/100 inventory. Its 20 train identities span five country-source families and four workflows (Calc growth, Calc risk, Impress deck, Writer brief), five identities per workflow. The 20 selection and 100 final candidate identities are rehashed and checked for source, template, and instance separation; they are not accessed by the train GUI actor.

The [current offline source-plan receipt](evidence/native-wdi-v066-train20-source-plan-2026-09-28.json) binds a private plan SHA-256 of `353b9878ddaf12fb954e7739e5f358d0fdc40a35e04cd0dc0e5521c499dfc5d2`. Two earlier local offline plan drafts were superseded after source review, before any provider intent; the current private plan is the only dispatch input. It fixes all 20 package, input, oracle, instruction, and action-script digests before a new guest. It assigns 15 train identities to scripted SFT demonstration collection and five to task-disjoint model holdout. Four holdouts are the complete set of workflows for one WDI country family; the fifth is from another country family. That second family is intentionally mixed between SFT and holdout, so the diagnostic is **task-disjoint with one fully source-family-disjoint stratum**, not a claim of complete source-family separation. The source assignment is deterministic from the original inventory hash, independent of outcomes. Task IDs, country choices, instructions, answers, and raw files remain evaluator-private.

For each of the 15 SFT identities, the collector requires a new exclusive intent before E2B creation. It checks AC power, active-zero provider state, the pinned guest image proxy and scoped LibreOffice profile, 8 GiB host free space, a 600-second full lease, and a maximum of 20 total intents at concurrency one. Every actor action uses the common v0.6.6 screenshot/action contract and is bounded to 32 steps. Observation and predispatch PNGs, any frame resamples, the action envelope, and the actor-saved XLSX/PPTX/DOCX are retained privately. A separate auditor reopens the raw bytes, rederives the action script and saved-artifact verdict, and requires verified guest teardown. A failed, partial, or uncertain identity stops the cursor. The controller has no automatic same-ID replay. The 20-lease conservative E2B quote is a scope bound, not an invoice or an arbitrary account spending cap. No paid 20-ID run has been dispatched by this plan.

After 15 distinct demonstrations pass independent audit, only those 15 may enter Qwen3.8-27B vision LoRA SFT through Tinker. The five holdout identities must remain inaccessible to the SFT exporter and checkpoint selector. Freeze the exact dataset hashes, renderer, LoRA rank/steps, seed, sampler prompt, and v0.6.6 action parser before any holdout sampling. Each holdout then receives one base and one LoRA attempt in separate fresh E2B Desktop guests with identical screenshot-only observations, instruction, 32-action limit, 600-second lease, and independent actor-saved OOXML verifier. Record paired exact completion, invalid action/timeout/failure codes, latency, token use, and actual provider and Tinker costs separately. A positive exploratory signal requires at least two net LoRA-only paired wins, no base-only regression, and no increase in invalid actions; all five pairs and failures must still be reported. Five tasks are too few for a population-level significance claim.

These original training tasks are single-edit exercises. A base-model ceiling is plausible, and an improvement on them would not establish the effect on harder multi-edit final tasks. If the five paired holdouts saturate or fail to distinguish models, the next preregistered diagnostic needs newly sourced, task-disjoint multi-step training/holdout cases; final candidates must never be repurposed as training data. Historical MEX Impress, Calc, and Writer GUI positives are separate source evidence and cannot be counted as any of the new 15 identities or as model gain.
