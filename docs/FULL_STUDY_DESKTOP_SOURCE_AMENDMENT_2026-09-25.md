# Native desktop source amendment — 2026-09-25

**Status: adopted provisional primary source before any official outcome; the native desktop cell remains unqualified.** The earlier plan named a generic `desktop-native` cell and screened OSWorld LibreOffice tasks. No official final task or researcher campaign has run on that cell. This amendment selects **original LibreOffice tasks built from World Development Indicators (WDI) observations** as the provisional primary source. Any eventual result must be labeled original EnvLoop WDI-derived LibreOffice tasks, never OSWorld scores or an OSWorld reproduction. OSWorld remains a quarantined development comparator.

## Reason for the source change

The [OSWorld cross-application audit](evidence/osworld-native-cross-app-controls-2026-09-25.md) found that 90/100 provisional evaluators require cloud gold files and 90/100 save the output during evaluator postconfiguration, which can mask a failure by the actor to save. The stock E2B LibreOffice image also reserialized original Impress shapes and paginated a third-party article differently from gold. Some source documents visibly carry third-party noncommercial/no-modification notices. Two genuine OSWorld development controls passed, but their evidence does not establish rights, evaluator compatibility, or 100 per-task admissions. Keeping those tasks as the primary 100 without resolving these issues would be misleading.

The [original task factory](../native_desktop_factory/README.md) instead authors input Office documents, actor instructions, and independent OOXML verifiers in this repository. It stages authentic country-year facts from one pinned [World Development Indicators](https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037712/world-development-indicators) snapshot. The catalog marks WDI public and [CC BY 4.0](https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/public-licenses). The 2026-09-25 snapshot has exactly 35 countries × five indicators × six years = 1,050 nonmissing observations, SHA-256 `cad6aafbd856ebb78f3f50106ab1b5b38ac369a2da251e2442268e296fde6d9f`. All operational requests, presentation designs, and derived calculations are EnvLoop-created simulations; WDI is the provenance of observations, not of work orders or conclusions.

## Proposed denominator and evidence boundary

The private country split yields 20 train, 20 selection, and **100 final candidates**, with 25 final countries × four workflows (50 Calc, 25 Impress, 25 Writer). The country is the prespecified primary source family for final uncertainty; four tasks per country are correlated. The private split-map hash and candidate-inventory hash are in the [field-limited development receipt](evidence/native-wdi-original-desktop-development-2026-09-25.json). The private mapping, final task instructions, initial Office documents, oracles, and screenshots must stay evaluator-owned until the final freeze/release decision. The WDI data source itself is public; the hidden boundary concerns task instances, defects, and feedback.

Nine separate E2B Desktop instances completed one train-only positive, one plausible near-miss, and one cold-reset control for each of Calc, Impress, and Writer. A tenth sandbox performed a neutral LibreOffice save to normalize the Impress input before its selected actor trio. All selected positive outputs were saved through the native GUI and passed independent artifact checks; all near-misses were saved through the GUI and rejected; all three fresh instances restored input bytes and were terminated. The original and saved train-only files are published for independent parser replay. The initial unnormalized Impress save changed untouched text-box geometry and is excluded from the selected controls; **all final Impress candidates still require equivalent normalization**. These are **development controls**, not official final IDs or model scores. The [per-ID gate](../native_desktop_factory/admit.py) currently has **0/100 admitted final tasks**, because no final candidate has its own GUI positive/negative/reset trio.

There is a second deliberate fail-closed condition. The present generator uses four **shared structural workflow templates** across train, selection, and final and changes edit count and data values. It labels the template groups semantically rather than falsely prefixing them by split. The current full-study contract requires template-family separation, so this candidate inventory cannot enter the frozen matrix. Two scientifically honest options remain: build genuinely distinct task structures per split, or preregister a within-template, country-held-out transfer study and update the validator before any campaign or final result. The latter would answer a narrower question about transfer across countries and would need explicit difficulty and leakage analysis. **The provisional source selection does not adopt either option or qualify the candidate inventory.**

## Admission rule before any official model run

For each of the 100 final identities: bind source and package hashes; document license; run a fresh native-GUI positive and plausible near-miss under the frozen screenshot/action contract; read the actor-saved OOXML before evaluator postprocessing; independently check target values/formulas, cached recalculation where applicable, and non-target preservation; destroy the sandbox and prove restoration in a distinct fresh sandbox; and record time, calls, failures, and cost. Then resolve the shared-template policy, establish full runtime/image/font/profile hashes and Qwen3.8 renderer/teacher/researcher budgets, and publish the pre-campaign freeze digest. Until then this is a **candidate desktop track with zero official outcomes**. The OSWorld tasks remain quarantined development comparators, not silently merged into this source.
