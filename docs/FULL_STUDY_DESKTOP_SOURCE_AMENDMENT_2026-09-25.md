# Native desktop source amendment — 2026-09-25

**Status: adopted provisional primary source before any official outcome; the native desktop cell remains unqualified.** The earlier plan named a generic `desktop-native` cell and screened OSWorld LibreOffice tasks. No official final task or researcher campaign has run on that cell. This amendment selects **original LibreOffice tasks built from World Development Indicators (WDI) observations** as the provisional primary source. Any eventual result must be labeled original EnvLoop WDI-derived LibreOffice tasks, never OSWorld scores or an OSWorld reproduction. OSWorld remains a quarantined development comparator.

## Reason for the source change

The [OSWorld cross-application audit](evidence/osworld-native-cross-app-controls-2026-09-25.md) found that 90/100 provisional evaluators require cloud gold files and 90/100 save the output during evaluator postconfiguration, which can mask a failure by the actor to save. The stock E2B LibreOffice image also reserialized original Impress shapes and paginated a third-party article differently from gold. Some source documents visibly carry third-party noncommercial/no-modification notices. Two genuine OSWorld development controls passed, but their evidence does not establish rights, evaluator compatibility, or 100 per-task admissions. Keeping those tasks as the primary 100 without resolving these issues would be misleading.

The [original task factory](../native_desktop_factory/README.md) instead authors input Office documents, actor instructions, and independent OOXML verifiers in this repository. It stages authentic country-year facts from one pinned [World Development Indicators](https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037712/world-development-indicators) snapshot. The catalog marks WDI public and [CC BY 4.0](https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/public-licenses). The 2026-09-25 snapshot has exactly 35 countries × five indicators × six years = 1,050 nonmissing observations, SHA-256 `cad6aafbd856ebb78f3f50106ab1b5b38ac369a2da251e2442268e296fde6d9f`. All operational requests, presentation designs, and derived calculations are EnvLoop-created simulations; WDI is the provenance of observations, not of work orders or conclusions.

## Proposed denominator and evidence boundary

The private country split yields 20 train, 20 selection, and **100 final candidates**, with 25 final countries × four workflows (50 Calc, 25 Impress, 25 Writer). The country is the prespecified primary source family for final uncertainty; four tasks per country are correlated. The private split-map hash and current v2 candidate-inventory hash are in the [field-limited v2 receipt](evidence/native-wdi-v2-final-gui-calibration-2026-09-25.json). The earlier [v1 development receipt](evidence/native-wdi-original-desktop-development-2026-09-25.json) remains historical evidence. The private mapping, final task instructions, initial Office documents, oracles, and screenshots must stay evaluator-owned until the final freeze/release decision. The WDI data source itself is public; the hidden boundary concerns task instances, defects, and feedback.

At the initial 2026-09-25 development stage, nine separate E2B Desktop instances completed one train-only positive, plausible near-miss, and cold-reset control for each of Calc, Impress, and Writer. A tenth sandbox performed a neutral LibreOffice save for the selected Impress actor trio. The original and saved train-only files are published for independent parser replay. The initial unnormalized Impress save changed untouched text-box geometry and is excluded. These were **development controls**, not official final IDs or model scores. The later 2026-09-27 full per-ID qualification is recorded below.

The first candidate generator used four **shared structural workflow templates** across train, selection, and final and changed only edit count and data values. It exposed the overlap through semantic template labels and failed the matrix gate. The [v2 factory](../native_desktop_factory/factory_v2.py) now implements physically and causally different split templates: Calc progresses from two to three to four sheets, Impress from four to five to seven slides, and final Writer cases add a second decision-rule table. The same private source-country split yields a new v2 candidate inventory. This resolves the specific shared-template naming/structure issue without relabeling the old files. It does **not** establish that the tasks are sufficiently difficult or that 100 final identities are qualified.

The first [v2 calibration receipt](evidence/native-wdi-v2-final-gui-calibration-2026-09-25.md) historically recorded **4/100** private final-ID GUI trios and one normalized Impress input. The [2026-09-27 full-scale audit](evidence/native-wdi-100-gui-calibration-2026-09-27.md) supersedes that engineering count: **100/100** task-specific GUI positive/near-miss/reset trios and **25/25** neutral-normalized final Impress inputs passed, across 25 held-out country source families. All prior failed and provider-invalid attempts remain preserved. The [fair saved-artifact scorer candidate](evidence/native-wdi-fair-scorer-freeze-2026-09-27.md) separately rechecked all 100 positives and 100 near-misses under a pre-result semantic rubric. The **official full-study admitted count remains 0/100** until the common Qwen action/observation/parser, complete runtime/font/profile, rights, cost, hidden-set and six-cell pre-campaign gates pass.

A [2026-09-27 training-only live smoke](evidence/native-wdi-qwen-v064-nonfinal-smoke-2026-09-27.md) then bound the shared Qwen v0.6.4 parser and vision renderer to the original native Desktop actor interface. The latest bounded attempt completed two real multimodal Qwen samples and applied two validated GUI clicks. Its two-action limit left the saved training document unchanged; the development verifier rejected it. No final task was sampled, and this does not admit an official model outcome.

The [two-sandbox runtime fingerprint](evidence/native-wdi-runtime-fingerprint-2026-09-27.md) found matching native binary, font-tree, fontconfig, OS, and package-manifest hashes, but the LibreOffice profile generated after the same neutral open differed bytewise. The installed E2B SDK exposes a template ID, not an image digest. These probes narrow the environment gap but **do not pass** the full image/profile freeze gate or change the zero official-outcome status.

A later [profile drift analysis](evidence/native-wdi-profile-drift-2026-09-27.md) isolated the cross-sandbox mismatch to two numeric prompt-timing fields in one registry XML file and demonstrated a matching restricted canonical snapshot across two more sandboxes. That is diagnostic evidence only: raw profile state continued to mutate during neutral open, and the image digest remains unavailable. The official image/profile gate stays failed.

## Admission rule before any official model run

For each of the 100 final identities: bind source and package hashes; document license; run a fresh native-GUI positive and plausible near-miss under the frozen screenshot/action contract; read the actor-saved OOXML before evaluator postprocessing; independently check target values/formulas, cached recalculation where applicable, and non-target preservation; destroy the sandbox and prove restoration in a distinct fresh sandbox; and record time, calls, failures, and cost. Those evaluator controls are now complete, but their evidence must be bound to the revised fair scorer, full runtime/image/font/profile hashes, Qwen3.8 renderer/teacher/researcher budgets, and shared v0.6.4 action parser before a pre-campaign freeze digest can be published. Until then this is a **candidate desktop track with zero official outcomes**. The OSWorld tasks remain quarantined development comparators, not silently merged into this source.
