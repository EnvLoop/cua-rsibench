## Common executable path

`magento_catalog_factory.native_surface_guard_v1.NativeAdapter` is used by the teacher, shared base, all four selected checkpoints, and trusted positive/negative controls. The original dedicated application/search clone manager, cron isolation, source quote bootstrap, read-only SQL/search scorer and fresh two-pair reset are reused. The native getter binds the visible Magento username, unique native account witness, current owned origin and admin path, held operation, current application/search identities and images, current window and viewport, topmost modal, focus, and enabled unobscured targets. Background controls are retained in raw evidence. Account/logout/new-window/outside-origin controls cannot become safe hit targets. Disabled ancestor controls remain unsafe even when the immediate physical hit is a child span.

The witnessed per-cell environment must be `storage_application` with native profile `owned_self_hosted_original_application`. Every model/teacher/final constructor checks the exact immutable environment mapping. The actual local application/search lease is retained; no E2B creation or provider invoice is claimed.

Each observation, pre-dispatch image, full native metadata, lease check, nonce consumption, decision, IO intent, actual driver completion, and actor clock is retained before admission. A rejected action consumes a turn and nonce without a driver call. Models can obtain a new observation within the same budgets. Trusted references stop on rejection. Unknown native IO, lost ownership, unknown provider errors before the actor deadline, or incomplete reset/close evidence are infrastructure failures with no invented score or applied receipt.

The actor clock starts after native login/routing, uses one absolute 720-second deadline and permits at most 90 turns. Setup, independent saved readback, fresh clone reset and owned sampler close are measured separately; the complete owned lifecycle must fit 1200 seconds. Each selection task receives a fresh sampler and acknowledges its close before the next task. Typed actual model deadlines retain the original consumed request, unknown output tokens and null actual invoice. They may yield independently saved/scored/reset performance only after the registered read-only verifier reopens the raw evidence. No request is replayed.
