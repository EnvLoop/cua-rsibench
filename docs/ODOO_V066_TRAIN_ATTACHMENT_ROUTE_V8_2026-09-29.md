The [v7 plan](ODOO_V066_TRAIN_ATTACHMENT_ROUTE_V7_2026-09-29.md) is marked superseded; its run directory remains absent.

The independent success auditor requires one guarded positive and one guarded negative double-click with phase, RFQ route, SKU/value hashes, DOM identity and physical-frame receipts. Other actions retain the existing Odoo RFQ and PDF-viewer guards.
