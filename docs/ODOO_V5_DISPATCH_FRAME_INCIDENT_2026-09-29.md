# Odoo v0.6.6 parse-border candidate: first GUI dispatch incident

The source-bound v5 no-GUI gate passed before the first fresh candidate GUI
attempt. The attempt is terminal and remains in its original private run
directory. Its [independent saved-evidence audit](evidence/odoo-v066-parse-border-v5-first-dispatch-terminal-failure-audit-2026-09-29.json)
reopens the frozen evaluator source, source and gate hashes, durable batch and
action intents, hash-chained journal, every relevant PNG, original worker
lease prefix, saved SQL and full physical filestore. The audit reports zero
model attempts and zero official final admissions.

Three navigation actions completed. At the source-document attachment click,
the parse-stage screenshot matched exactly and a durable step-3 action intent
was written. The dispatch guard then recorded six identical physical PNGs
that differed from the observed screenshot at only the two pinned RFQ tab
border pixels. Its final physical read returned **exactly to the observed
screenshot**. The frozen guard required the final PNG to equal the preceding
alternate, so it returned false before the browser `mouse.click` branch. The
frozen final-sample label is `third_or_material_frame_rejected`; independent
pixel readback identifies an exact observed-frame return, not a third or
materially changed image. No step-3 result, opened source-document evidence,
or positive saved business state exists. The original worker SQL and full
filestore were restored exactly; both Odoo services stopped and the lease was
released. No action is replayed after a durable intent.

For a future source epoch, a longer exact-return wait is a small change but
can still reject a stable alternate. Masking the two pixels for every action
would broaden the contract across unrelated routes and controls. A narrower
v6 design would accept the final PNG only if it is one of the two already
proven full-frame images, `{observed, one two-pixel alternate}`. It must take
a second final physical read immediately before dispatch and require the same
member of that set, unchanged URL, task binding, frame identity, and visible
target-control identity and bounds outside the border. Any third PNG, target
change, or second-read mismatch fails closed. This is a design proposal, not
a patched result or an authorization to retry. A new source freeze, fresh
candidate nonce, independent verifier, and no-GUI baseline gate must precede
any future GUI attempt.
