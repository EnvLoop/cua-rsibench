# Odoo current-source evaluator-control adoption proposal

The v0.6.6 validator-corrected Odoo source freeze has 25 unchanged current
source hashes and retains three terminal failed selection controls. Its
20-selection and 100-hidden split plans are still bound to the earlier
six-cell ratification. The [current six-cell source candidate](../FULL_STUDY_V066_CURRENT_SIX_CELL_SOURCE_CANDIDATE_2026-09-29.md)
records the changed Odoo adapter, but has no live-dispatch authority. Updating
the old plan's ratification hash in place would erase the history of what was
actually frozen before those controls.

The [field-limited adoption proposal](../evidence/odoo-v066-current-candidate-control-adoption-proposal-2026-09-29.json)
binds the new candidate, the validator-corrected freeze, both historical
split plans, and all three public failure audits by SHA-256. It records the
historical and current ratification hashes separately. Its validator rereads
the current source and upstream receipts, rejects a relabeled old plan or a
candidate claiming dispatch authority, and reports zero new controls,
admissions, campaigns, and model attempts. It does not read private task
instructions or create any service, sandbox, or model call.

A fourth same-ID selection control needs a separate dated evaluator-only
source epoch, new split-local private/public plans with an explicit current
candidate adoption field, and a fresh private run directory with a random
nonce. The source epoch must preserve the three failures, prohibit automatic
replay, and bind the v0.6.6 validator and pinned-border dispatch bytes. A
no-GUI gate must independently re-audit all three retained failures, acquire
the original selection worker lease, compare current SQL and the full physical
filestore against the frozen baseline, and restore the stopped service state.
Only after this gate passes on AC power may one bounded current-profile GUI
control be attempted. Its raw screenshot/action record, saved-state positive
and negative scores, and cold reset require independent review before any
selection or hidden batch advances.

This proposal cannot become the 24-campaign study ratification. That later
artifact needs all six live source/runtime controls, 600 individually
qualified final identities, a frozen common model/action/budget protocol, and
an immutable pre-campaign witness. Three offline tests verify this proposal's
current-source boundary and rejection of a relabeled plan and an unauthorized
candidate. No fourth Odoo GUI attempt has been made.
