# GitLab final-candidate admission preflight

**Scope:** A read-only bridge between 100 retained evaluator GUI trios and the
separate `cua-final-cell-v0.6` qualification gate. It runs before any official
model result. It cannot create a qualified manifest or dispatch an actor.

Three possible approaches were considered. Reusing the existing aggregate
100-case receipt alone would not bind each candidate to its raw files. Emitting
100 reset/verifier proofs directly from that aggregate would overstate what the
retained development controls establish. The chosen approach independently
reopens every current case receipt, persisted-state readback, screenshot hash,
and failure history; it records a private identity and evidence map with
explicit admission blockers and publishes only counts and digests.

The preflight regenerates the private CISA-derived world from its evaluator
seed, verifies the reserve family and exposure quarantine, then checks all
20 train, 20 selection, and 100 provisional final identities for source,
template, and instance separation. It compares the mutable GUI index against
the retained append-only failure ledger and dated requalification audit. For
each current final task, it requires the latest whole GUI trio to match the
index, three original application case receipts, 1/0/1 independent readbacks,
three fresh resets, all retained screenshot hashes, and the exact baseline
business digest. Any missing or changed byte aborts the whole preflight.

The private output includes provisional task identities and hashes of the raw
per-task evidence. The public projection has no task IDs, project paths,
advisories, instructions, gold, screenshot paths, or credentials. It reports
zero official admissions and blocks creating a qualified v0.6 manifest until
the cell has task-level reset/verifier proofs under the exact v0.6 schema, a
live v0.6.6 student observation/sampling qualification, frozen complete
runtime and action bindings, and the six-cell pre-campaign witness. The
historical deterministic failures remain in the denominator.
