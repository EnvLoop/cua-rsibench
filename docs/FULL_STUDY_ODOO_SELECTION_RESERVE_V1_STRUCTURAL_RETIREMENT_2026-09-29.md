# Odoo selection reserve v1 structural retirement — 2026-09-29

The first prospective five-case reserve cohort was generated and independently
audited **offline only**. No source PDF was opened in Odoo, no saved
application state was scored, no model saw a task, and no candidate was
admitted. Visual and structural review found that each v1 document directly
singled out the one corrected line. The retired selection cases required
reading a three-line source and comparing all three RFQ lines to find two
same-line mismatches. V1 therefore reduced source-reading demand and does not
meet the frozen difficulty-matching requirement. Its five identities and
packages remain evaluator-private development material and will never be
promoted or replayed as selection tasks.

This is a pre-GUI, pre-model structural rejection of the entire cohort, not
task-by-task selection on outcomes. A new v2 freeze must bind a fresh private
seed, five new identities, and the v1 package commitments before any v2
candidate is materialized. Every v2 source must show all three lines with
their quantities, USD unit rates, and promised dates, without identifying the
faulty line in the actor-visible document or prompt. It must preserve two
same-line target edits, the same native Purchase workflow, a nearby wrong RFQ,
and strict no-regression/reset requirements. The declared 20-task selection
denominator and all 100 final identities remain unchanged. The shared native
attachment viewer still requires live original-application qualification.
