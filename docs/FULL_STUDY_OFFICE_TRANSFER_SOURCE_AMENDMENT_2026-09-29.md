## Additional SEC Excel training lane

The [140-case aggregate](evidence/sec-private-full-offline-140-2026-09-27.md) commits to 13 final semantic graph types but deliberately omits the graph-to-primitive dependency map. The local evaluator-private 140-slot registry has its expected published SHA-256 and 20/20/100 counts; that registry supplies graph reservations and split metadata, **not** the causal skill descriptions needed to author a legitimate analogue. The [field-limited gate receipt](evidence/office-excel-transfer-source-gate-2026-09-29.json) therefore reports `blocked` and zero analogue coverage. We will not infer the 13 graphs from names, reuse final workbooks, or equate a public development workflow with a private final graph.

Before Excel transfer materialization, an evaluator must make a private, independently reviewed skill card for **each exact final graph reservation**. Each card describes only causal skill atoms, dependency edges, and minimum target-edit depth, without per-ID issuer, accession, task text, workbook, or answer. The first train-only diagnostic stage needs **two independently sourced analogues per graph (26 total)**, from two new issuers per graph. After valid train-only paired saved-state observations justify expansion, the full target is eight per graph (**104 total**). The initial 26 cases are enough to screen for an obvious action-contract floor or ceiling; they do not establish a model-improvement effect. Every analogue must use an issuer CIK, original 10-K accession and authored template family distinct from all 140 anchors. Each case must preserve exact-fact source verification, its separate scenario label, a reviewed verifier, a saved positive, a one-target-short near miss, hardcode and collateral-source rejections, source and scenario counterfactuals, and a fresh reset. The actual target count must meet its private skill card; nine edits in the first final staging plan is **not** a universal requirement for the other 12 graphs.

Excel expansion must retain all 26 frozen pilot case metadata records byte for byte, including source, skill signature, template and artifact commitments. The source contract rejects any omission or rewrite before considering the additional 78 cases.

The schema gate checks these declarations and source/template disjointness but does not replay private `.xlsx` artifacts. Even a metadata-complete output remains `artifact_replay_pending`, with zero independently replayed controls. A separate independent saved-OOXML oracle review and original Excel-for-the-web save/download/reset controls are required before any case enters researcher training. SEC filing facts are sourced from original public EDGAR filings; the workbook, fault injection, and sensitivities are authored. The intended distribution tier is **derived filing facts and authored scenarios only**, not redistribution of full original filing documents. No SEC endorsement is implied.

This source-only amendment is frozen before final model outcomes. Its blocked states cannot be converted into score denominators or a transfer-effect claim. The public receipt exports counts, status, rights tier and source commitments only; private graph names, issuers, accessions, workbook paths and gold remain evaluator-held.
