## Additional PowerPoint training lane

[`office_transfer_source_gate_v1.py`](../tools/office_transfer_source_gate_v1.py) requires eight **new** official World Bank WDI country CSV ZIP extracts with 30 exact 2019–2024 observations each. Each ZIP must retain its private source snapshot and provenance (download date, data-update date, CC BY 4.0 catalog license, ZIP and snapshot hashes). The gate rejects any country already in the original 35-country partition, the eight-country calibration inventory, active selection/final, all 12 historical final-plan revisions, or the future reserve queue. It binds the unchanged v13 20/20/100 plan. The generator then prepares ten causal workflows × eight distinct country source families = **80 four-target train-only specifications**. Each uses the same required causal correction depth as a final task but a separate authored training deck template, task identity, source, and visual styling. The presentation builder accepts only this train-only role.

The generator can optionally materialize all 80 editable decks locally. Its offline gate reopens the original source facts, verifies the native chart and embedded workbook, package integrity and layout, then independently rejects partial, collateral-content and chart-damage near misses against each full positive. Only a complete 80/80 direct-file control receipt can be described as an *offline* training inventory. A PowerPoint-for-the-web saved/downloaded positive, near miss, and fresh reset remains a separate admission for any GUI training use. WDI numeric facts are licensed CC BY 4.0; the analyst briefs, committee rules, errors, and distinct training template are EnvLoop-authored simulations. No final country names or answers enter the public receipt.

The [source preflight receipt](evidence/office-ppt-transfer-source-gate-2026-09-29.json) binds the active v13 plan hash and records that the eight additional country CSVs and evaluator-private 80-case calibration manifest were **not supplied to this gate**. The new generator has not been run against real new sources. Accordingly the additional PPT pool is **0 source-admitted / 0 offline-controlled / 0 GUI-admitted** today. The existing calibration 80 remains intact and separately classified. The code's synthetic unit tests verify the 10×8 allocation, four faulted target fields, source/template isolation, rights tier, and field-limited receipt; they are not evidence of 80 new real decks.

## Additional SEC Excel training lane

The [140-case aggregate](evidence/sec-private-full-offline-140-2026-09-27.md) commits to 13 final semantic graph types but deliberately omits the graph-to-primitive dependency map. The local evaluator-private 140-slot registry has its expected published SHA-256 and 20/20/100 counts; that registry supplies graph reservations and split metadata, **not** the causal skill descriptions needed to author a legitimate analogue. The [field-limited gate receipt](evidence/office-excel-transfer-source-gate-2026-09-29.json) therefore reports `blocked` and zero analogue coverage. We will not infer the 13 graphs from names, reuse final workbooks, or equate a public development workflow with a private final graph.

Before Excel transfer materialization, an evaluator must make a private, independently reviewed skill card for **each exact final graph reservation**. Each card describes only causal skill atoms, dependency edges, and minimum target-edit depth, without per-ID issuer, accession, task text, workbook, or answer. A separate private train-only pool must then supply at least eight independently sourced analogues per graph (**104 minimum**), from at least two new issuers per graph, with issuer CIK, original 10-K accession and authored template family distinct from all 140 anchors. Each case must preserve exact-fact source verification, its separate scenario label, a reviewed verifier, a saved positive, an eight-or-more-edit near miss where appropriate, hardcode and collateral-source rejections, source and scenario counterfactuals, and a fresh reset. The actual target count must meet its private skill card; nine edits in the first final staging plan is **not** a universal requirement for the other 12 graphs.

The schema gate checks these declarations and source/template disjointness but does not replay private `.xlsx` artifacts. Even a metadata-complete output remains `artifact_replay_pending`, with zero independently replayed controls. A separate independent saved-OOXML oracle review and original Excel-for-the-web save/download/reset controls are required before any case enters researcher training. SEC filing facts are sourced from original public EDGAR filings; the workbook, fault injection, and sensitivities are authored. The intended distribution tier is **derived filing facts and authored scenarios only**, not redistribution of full original filing documents. No SEC endorsement is implied.

This source-only amendment is frozen before final model outcomes. Its blocked states cannot be converted into score denominators or a transfer-effect claim. The public receipt exports counts, status, rights tier and source commitments only; private graph names, issuers, accessions, workbook paths and gold remain evaluator-held.
